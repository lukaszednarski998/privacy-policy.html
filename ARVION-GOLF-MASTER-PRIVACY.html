Privacy Policy — English

1. Publisher and contact

ARVION / APLIKACJE ANDROID (“we”, “us”) publishes ARVION GOLF MASTER for Android phones and Wear OS watches. For questions about privacy or requests concerning data under the developer's control, contact lukaszbednarski998@interia.pl. Website: ARVION official site.

2. Game information stored on a device

Depending on the installed version and features you use, the game may save local gameplay and preference information, such as scores, number of strokes, completed holes, selected courses or Mini Golf levels, unlocked equipment, settings, selected language and progress. These records are used to operate the game and remember your preferences. Ordinary play does not require you to create an ARVION account or enter your real name, postal address or email address.

The game's intended design uses device-local storage rather than a developer-operated player-profile service. If a later release introduces developer-hosted online storage or account functionality, this notice will be revised to explain it before that processing begins.

3. Trial and purchase entitlement, if enabled

Where an optional trial is offered, the game may retain local information needed to measure the trial period and its availability, such as installation or trial timestamps and an expiration flag. Where an optional full-version purchase is offered, the game may store a local entitlement flag and technical purchase status to recognise access to the purchased content. The exact trial implementation and any device identifiers used must be checked against the release build.

4. Google Play purchases

If a one-time in-app purchase or purchase restoration is available, Google Play Billing handles the payment flow. Google may process account, payment, device and transaction information under its own policies. The application may receive purchase status and technical transaction identifiers needed to validate and restore access. The developer does not receive a player's full payment-card number from the game. Purchase restoration across devices, if supported, depends on Google Play and the eligible Google account.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Device permissions and services

Normal golf gameplay does not require access to your contacts, photographs, camera, microphone or precise location. Depending on the release version, the app may use haptic feedback and Google Play's billing service. The final set of permissions is defined by the Android manifest of the released application and should be checked before publication.

6. Advertising, analytics and sharing

The intended version does not rely on third-party advertising or an ARVION-operated analytics/tracking service. Local scores and game preferences are not intended for sale or for disclosure to advertising partners. This does not affect technical information that Android, Wear OS, Google Play, or the store itself may process in their own capacity; their privacy policies apply separately. Any new advertising, analytics or data-sharing integration will require an update to this policy before release.

7. Purposes and legal basis

Local game data is used to provide the gameplay and save settings you choose to use. Where EU/EEA data protection law applies and a lawful basis is required, relevant processing may be necessary to provide the requested service; purchase-related administration may also support our legitimate interests in protecting access to paid features and complying with applicable obligations. Google independently determines the legal basis for processing within its own services.

8. Retention, removal and backups

Local records generally remain on your device until cleared by you, the operating system, or uninstalling the application. Clearing app data or uninstalling may erase local progress and preferences. Android's optional system backup or device-transfer features may preserve certain app data separately according to your device and account settings. Google Play transaction records are controlled by Google and are not deleted simply by uninstalling the game.

9. Security and children

Device-local records are generally protected by Android/Wear OS application storage mechanisms. No technical measure offers absolute protection. ARVION GOLF MASTER is not intended to solicit children's personal contact details; caregivers can use Google Play family controls for purchases and downloads.

10. Your rights

Where applicable, you may request information about personal data processed under the developer's control and seek access, correction, erasure, restriction or objection, subject to legal exceptions. You can also manage or erase local game information through your device settings. Contact us at the address above. You may lodge a complaint with your competent data-protection authority.

11. Hosting of this policy page

This public document is hosted through GitHub/GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical web-request data, such as IP addresses, to serve and secure the page under GitHub's privacy statement. This page does not intentionally include advertising pixels or third-party analytics scripts.

12. Changes

This policy may change as the app is developed or if data processing, integrations or legal requirements change. The version and date shown here will be updated. Before a production release, the policy must be checked against the actual app manifest, dependencies and data flows.
