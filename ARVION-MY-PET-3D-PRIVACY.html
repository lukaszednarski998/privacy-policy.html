Privacy Policy - ARVION MY PET 3D

Effective date: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to ARVION MY PET 3D for Android phones and Wear OS smartwatches, package name com.arvion.pet3d.

Summary: ARVION MY PET 3D is designed to keep gameplay data locally on the user's devices and to synchronize the same virtual pet and game progress between a paired Android phone and Wear OS smartwatch. The app does not require an ARVION account, does not contain advertising, and does not include developer-operated analytics or cross-app tracking.

1. Data collection

ARVION MY PET 3D does not require users to create an ARVION account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as the user's real name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone recordings, health information, or advertising identifiers for gameplay purposes.

The developer does not operate a server-side user-profile database for ARVION MY PET 3D.

2. Local game data

The app stores game information locally on the user's phone and/or smartwatch so that gameplay can continue between sessions. This may include the selected pet, a custom pet name entered by the user, pet status and needs, level and experience, selected room, gameplay settings, action cooldowns, timestamps used to update the virtual pet over time, and information needed to manage the trial and unlocked state.

This information is used to provide the game and is not intentionally transmitted to the developer's own servers.

3. Phone and Wear OS synchronization

When the user pairs the Android phone and Wear OS smartwatch and uses synchronization, ARVION MY PET 3D may transfer game-state information between those paired devices so that the same pet and progress are available on both devices.

Synchronization data may include the selected pet, pet name, pet status and needs, level and experience, gameplay state, room/settings information, timestamps, and trial/unlock state required to keep the two installations consistent.

The app is designed to use Android/Wear OS platform communication services for this device-to-device functionality. The developer does not use a separate ARVION cloud account or developer-operated cloud database to store this synchronized game progress.

4. 60-minute trial

ARVION MY PET 3D may provide a limited free trial period of approximately 60 minutes from first installation. The app stores information needed to determine the trial state, such as installation/trial timestamps and entitlement state. When phone-watch synchronization is available, relevant trial state may also be synchronized between the paired devices to prevent inconsistent trial periods.

5. One-time purchase and Google Play Billing

After the trial period, the user may choose to unlock the full game with a one-time purchase through Google Play. Payment processing is handled by Google Play Billing. ARVION MY PET 3D does not receive or store the user's payment-card details.

The app may receive purchase and entitlement information from Google Play, such as whether the full version has been purchased and technical purchase identifiers needed to confirm or restore access. This information is used only to provide the purchased game entitlement.

Google may process purchase and account information independently under Google's own privacy terms and Google Play policies.

6. Permissions

The app is designed to request only permissions and platform capabilities necessary for its Android, Wear OS, synchronization, and Google Play Billing functionality. Core virtual-pet gameplay does not require access to contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, photos, or health data.

7. Advertising, analytics and tracking

ARVION MY PET 3D does not contain third-party advertising, behavioral advertising, developer-operated analytics, or cross-app advertising tracking.

8. Generative AI

ARVION MY PET 3D does not provide or use a generative AI service as part of its core game functionality.

9. Sharing and sale of personal data

The developer does not sell or rent personal data collected through ARVION MY PET 3D. The app is designed so that normal game progress remains on the user's devices or is transferred between the user's paired phone and smartwatch rather than being stored in a developer-operated online account.

10. Children's privacy

The app does not intentionally request personal information from children. Users should not enter personal or sensitive information as a pet name or in any other user-editable field.

11. Data retention and deletion

Game progress, settings, synchronization state, trial information, and locally stored entitlement information remain on the user's device unless removed through available device/app-data controls or by uninstalling the application. Because the developer does not operate a server-side ARVION MY PET 3D profile database, the developer does not retain a separate server-side copy of normal game progress.

Google Play may retain transaction information according to Google's own policies and legal obligations.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights concerning their personal data, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and other rights provided by local privacy law. ARVION MY PET 3D is designed so that the developer does not receive normal local game-progress data. For privacy questions or requests relating to the app, please use the official developer contact information displayed on the ARVION MY PET 3D Google Play listing.

13. Platform services

Google Play, Google Play Billing, Google Play services, Android, and Wear OS may process technical, device, account, security, installation, synchronization, or transaction information independently when providing their platform services. Such processing is governed by the relevant Google terms and privacy policies and is separate from data processing performed directly by the developer.

14. Security

ARVION MY PET 3D is designed to minimize data processing by keeping normal game progress local and limiting synchronization to the functionality needed to keep the user's paired devices consistent. No method of electronic storage or communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality, platform services, purchase system, synchronization behavior, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.

16. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to ARVION MY PET 3D, please use the official developer contact information shown on the app's Google Play listing.
