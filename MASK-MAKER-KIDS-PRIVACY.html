Privacy Policy - Mask Maker Kids

Effective date: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Mask Maker Kids, an Android application with package name pl.arvion.maskmakerkids.

Summary: Mask Maker Kids is designed as a creative, child-friendly mask-making app. Projects are stored locally on the user's device. The app does not contain advertising, does not use developer-operated analytics or cross-app tracking, and does not require users to create an account.

1. Information collected by the app

Mask Maker Kids does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as a user's real name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, health information, advertising identifiers, camera images, or microphone recordings as part of its core functionality.

The developer does not operate a server-side user profile database for Mask Maker Kids.

2. Local project data

The app stores creative project data locally on the device so users can save and continue their work. This may include mask templates, colors, textures, stickers, drawing strokes, layer positions, scale, rotation, project names, print settings, and timestamps related to saved projects.

This information is used only to provide the app's mask creation, editing, saving, export, and printing features.

3. Photos, camera, microphone, location, and contacts

Version 1.0 of Mask Maker Kids does not require access to the device camera, microphone, precise location, or contacts for its core features.

The app does not provide a public photo gallery, social feed, chat system, or public profile feature.

4. Printing and PDF generation

Mask Maker Kids can create printable mask designs and PDF documents at the user's request. Generated files may be saved locally on the device, printed using the Android Print Framework, or shared using Android's system sharing features.

When the user chooses to print or share a file, the selected printer service, print provider, file provider, or sharing app may process the file independently under its own privacy policy. Mask Maker Kids does not automatically upload generated masks or PDFs to a developer-operated server.

5. Image export

The app may allow users to export their mask designs as image files. Exported files are created only after user action and may be stored in device storage or shared through Android's system sharing tools.

6. 60-minute trial

Mask Maker Kids may provide a free trial period of approximately 60 minutes from first installation. The app stores technical information locally that is needed to determine the trial state, such as installation/trial timestamps and entitlement status.

The trial continues according to elapsed time even if the app is placed in the background, closed, or the device is restarted.

7. One-time purchase and Google Play Billing

After the trial period, users may choose to unlock the full version with a one-time purchase through Google Play. The product is a non-consumable full-version unlock.

Payment processing is handled by Google Play Billing. Mask Maker Kids does not receive or store payment-card details.

The app may receive technical purchase and entitlement information from Google Play, such as whether the full version has been purchased and information required to confirm or restore access.

Google may process account, transaction, device, security, and billing information independently under Google's own terms and privacy policies.

8. Language and app preferences

The app may store local preferences such as the selected language, editor settings, print settings, and other options necessary for normal use. These preferences remain on the device unless removed through the device's app-data controls or by uninstalling the application.

9. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Mask Maker Kids does not contain third-party advertising.

Mask Maker Kids does not use developer-operated behavioral analytics, advertising profiling, or cross-app tracking.

The app is not designed to sell or rent user data.

10. Children's privacy

Mask Maker Kids is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. The core creative features work without requiring a child to create an account or provide personal contact details.

Parents and guardians should supervise use of device-level sharing and printing features where appropriate, because those features may send a generated file to a third-party app, service, printer, or provider selected by the user.

11. Data sharing

The developer does not sell or rent personal data collected through Mask Maker Kids.

Data may leave the app only when necessary to provide a feature explicitly initiated by the user, such as Google Play Billing, printing, saving/exporting a file, or sharing a file through Android's system interface.

12. Data retention and deletion

Saved projects, local settings, trial information, and locally stored entitlement information remain on the device until removed through available app/device controls or by uninstalling the app, subject to Android system behavior.

Files exported by the user, such as images or PDFs, may remain in device storage after the app is uninstalled until the user deletes them.

Google Play may retain purchase and transaction records according to Google's own policies and legal obligations.

13. Security

Mask Maker Kids is designed to minimize unnecessary data processing by keeping creative projects local to the device. No method of electronic storage or communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Parental controls

Where available, parent-focused sections of the app may use a simple parental gate before presenting purchase, restore, privacy, reset, or related settings. This feature is intended to reduce accidental access by children but is not a substitute for device-level parental controls.

15. Permissions

Mask Maker Kids is designed to request only permissions and platform capabilities needed for its features. The application does not require location, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions for the core version 1.0 experience.

Printing, file export, sharing, and billing are handled through Android and Google Play platform APIs where applicable.

16. Third-party platform services

Google Play, Google Play Billing, Android, device manufacturers, print services, and apps selected by the user for sharing may independently process technical, device, account, transaction, printing, or file-sharing information when providing their services.

Such processing is governed by the relevant third-party terms and privacy policies and is separate from processing performed directly by the developer.

17. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights concerning personal data, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or other rights provided by local privacy law.

Because Mask Maker Kids is designed not to maintain a developer-operated user account or cloud project database, the developer may not possess locally stored projects or exported files. For privacy questions or requests relating to the app, please use the official developer contact information displayed on the Mask Maker Kids Google Play listing.

18. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality, billing, printing, storage behavior, platform services, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.

19. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to Mask Maker Kids, please use the official developer contact information shown on the app's Google Play listing.
