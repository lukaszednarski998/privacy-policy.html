Privacy Policy — WORLD HOCKEY 3D

Effective date: 23 November 2026. Publisher: Łukasz Bednarski STUDIO / ARVION. This policy covers the Android phone and standalone Wear OS editions, package com.arvion.worldhockey3d, and the separate local tester edition com.arvion.worldhockey3d.tester.

Contact

Privacy questions: lukaszbednarski998@interia.pl. You do not need to send a purchase token or tester PIN when contacting support.

Game data stored on your device

The game stores scores, tournament progress, an unfinished match, challenge completion, language, sound, sensitivity and camera preferences in its private app storage. Trial timestamps, the highest observed clock value and a locally cached purchase entitlement are also stored there. The Android app-scoped device identifier may be read locally to check integrity of trial state; the game does not send that identifier to a developer server. The tester edition additionally stores its failed-attempt count and local tester unlock. These records are not sent to ARVION. The game does not include advertising, analytics or a developer-operated account or backend.

Google Play purchases

The application includes Google Play Billing Library 9.1.0 for an optional one-time, non-consumable full-version purchase and restoration. Google Play processes the payment, account and transaction information under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The application receives product identifiers, purchase status, acknowledgement status and a purchase token from the Play service and uses them to manage access. It does not receive payment card details. Purchase tokens are passed back to Google Play to acknowledge purchases; this version does not send them to a developer server.

A configured product identifier in source code does not mean a product is already available in Google Play. Availability depends on the publisher's Play Console configuration and release.

Permissions and device independence

No location, microphone, camera, contacts or photo access is requested. The Wear OS game runs on the watch without sending gameplay data to a paired phone. Internet access may be needed by Google Play for purchase checks. Normal gameplay uses local assets and runs offline.

Retention, deletion and backup

Local game records remain until removed through Android app data controls or uninstallation, subject to the operating system's own behavior. Application cloud backup is disabled in this release. Clearing local data can erase progress and trial/tester records; the application does not claim an uninstall-proof offline trial. Google retains transaction records under its own policies. Uninstalling the game does not delete your Google purchase history. Contact Google Play for payment record, refund and account requests.

Security and third parties

Android app storage isolation protects local records under normal device operation. Client-only access checks cannot provide absolute protection on modified devices. The developer does not sell game data. Google Play and, if you open this page, GitHub Pages process service traffic under their own policies. This policy page uses no advertising scripts or analytics.

Google Privacy Policy · GitHub Privacy Statement

Children and updates

The game has no in-game chat, public user profiles or user-generated uploads. Google Play may apply its own account and purchase safeguards. The intended audience and content rating are specified by the publisher in Google Play. This policy will be updated if data handling changes.

Dane przechowywane lokalnie

Zakupy Google Play

Aplikacja zawiera Google Play Billing Library 9.1.0 do opcjonalnego jednorazowego zakupu pełnej wersji oraz przywracania zakupu. Google Play przetwarza dane płatności, konta i transakcji zgodnie ze swoimi zasadami. Aplikacja otrzymuje identyfikator produktu, stan zakupu i potwierdzenia oraz token zakupu w celu zarządzania dostępem. Nie otrzymuje danych karty płatniczej. Token jest przekazywany z powrotem do Google Play w celu potwierdzenia odbioru zakupu; ta wersja nie wysyła go na serwer wydawcy.

Identyfikator wpisany w kodzie nie oznacza utworzenia aktywnego produktu w sklepie. Dostępność zależy od konfiguracji Play Console i wydania aplikacji.

Przechowywanie, usuwanie i kopie

Odinstalowanie gry nie usuwa historii zakupów Google. Wnioski o dane płatności, zwroty i konto należy kierować do Google Play.
