Privacy Policy - ARVION Watch Faces

Effective date: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to ARVION Watch Faces for Android phones and Wear OS smartwatches, package name com.arvion.watchfaces.

Summary: ARVION Watch Faces is a free companion/marketplace application that lets users browse, purchase with one-time Google Play purchases, synchronize and install ARVION watch faces and games on a paired Wear OS smartwatch. The app does not contain advertising or subscriptions, does not require an ARVION account, and does not use developer-operated analytics, advertising tracking or a developer-operated cloud profile database.

1. Information collected by the developer

ARVION Watch Faces does not require users to create an ARVION account and does not intentionally collect the user's real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, precise location, advertising identifiers, or payment-card details for the developer's own use.

The developer does not operate a server-side account or profile database for ARVION Watch Faces.

2. Local app data

The app stores limited information locally on the phone and/or smartwatch in order to provide its functionality. This may include language and interface preferences, selected watch face, transfer status, identifiers of purchased/unlocked products, synchronization state, and game settings or local game progress.

This local data is used to operate the app and is not intentionally uploaded to developer-operated servers.

3. Google Play Billing and one-time purchases

Watch faces and games available inside ARVION Watch Faces may be purchased as one-time digital products through Google Play Billing. The main ARVION Watch Faces application is free. The app does not use subscriptions and does not contain advertising.

Google Play processes payments. ARVION Watch Faces does not receive or store full payment-card details. The app may receive purchase-related information from Google Play, such as product identifiers, purchase state and entitlement information needed to unlock, restore and synchronize purchased content.

Google may process account, transaction, security and payment information independently under Google's own terms and privacy policies.

4. Phone and Wear OS synchronization

When the user connects a compatible Wear OS smartwatch, the phone app and watch app exchange technical information required for synchronization. This may include the list of purchased product identifiers, selected watch-face identifiers, installation/transfer status and control messages needed to install or activate a watch face or unlock a purchased game.

Signed watch-face packages may be transferred directly from the phone to the paired smartwatch using Android/Wear OS communication services, including Wi-Fi when available and Wear OS data/channel services as a fallback.

This communication is intended for the user's paired devices. The developer does not operate a separate ARVION cloud service for storing the user's watch-face purchases or synchronization history.

5. Watch-face data and complications

Some ARVION watch faces can display information supplied by Wear OS or by complication/data providers, such as time, date, battery status, steps, heart rate, weather, temperature, UV information, moon phase or other complication data selected or made available by the platform.

Such information is used to display the selected watch face on the user's smartwatch. ARVION Watch Faces is designed so that this watch-face/complication data is processed on the device and is not transmitted to developer-operated servers.

Availability of particular data depends on the smartwatch, Wear OS version, granted platform permissions and the data provider chosen by the user or system.

6. Nearby devices, Wi-Fi and network access

The app may use network-state, Wi-Fi-state, internet and nearby-Wi-Fi capabilities to communicate with Google Play services and to support faster transfer of purchased watch faces between the user's phone and paired smartwatch.

The nearby-Wi-Fi capability is not used by the developer to determine or collect the user's physical location. Where supported by Android, the permission is declared as not being used for location.

7. Notifications, boot and vibration

Included watch games may use vibration for gameplay feedback. ARVION My Pet 3D may optionally use notifications and boot-completed handling to provide local pet/game reminders after the user has enabled the relevant notification capability.

These functions are performed on the user's device and are not used for advertising or tracking.

8. Advertising, analytics and tracking

ARVION Watch Faces does not contain third-party advertising, behavioral advertising, developer-operated analytics, cross-app advertising tracking, or advertising SDKs.

9. Sale or sharing of personal data

The developer does not sell or rent personal data collected through ARVION Watch Faces. The app is designed to keep normal app preferences, entitlement cache and game progress on the user's devices and to transfer synchronization information only as needed between the paired phone and smartwatch and platform services required to provide purchases and Wear OS functionality.

10. Children's privacy

ARVION Watch Faces does not intentionally request personal information from children. Users should not enter personal or sensitive information into any optional user-editable game field.

11. Data retention and deletion

Local app settings, synchronization state and game progress remain on the user's device until removed through Android/Wear OS app-data controls or by uninstalling the application. Locally cached purchase entitlements may be recreated from Google Play after reinstalling or restoring the app.

Because the developer does not operate an ARVION server-side user profile database for this app, the developer does not retain a separate server-side copy of normal local app or game progress.

Google Play and other Google platform services may retain transaction, account or technical information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

12. Security

ARVION Watch Faces is designed to minimize data collection and to use Android, Wear OS and Google Play platform services for device communication and purchases. No method of electronic storage or communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Third-party platform services

Google Play, Google Play Billing, Google Play services, Android and Wear OS may independently process technical, device, account, security, installation, synchronization or transaction information when providing their platform services. Such processing is governed by the relevant Google terms and privacy policies.

14. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights concerning personal data, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and other rights provided by local law. For privacy questions or requests relating to ARVION Watch Faces, please use the official developer contact information displayed on the application's Google Play listing.

15. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality, purchase system, Wear OS integration, platform services or legal requirements change. The current version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.

16. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to ARVION Watch Faces, please use the official developer contact information shown on the ARVION Watch Faces Google Play listing.
