Privacy Policy

Updated: 1 November 2026 · com.aplikacjeandroid.arvionbasket

About the game and developer

This policy applies to ARVION BASKET, a standalone basketball game for Wear OS, package com.aplikacjeandroid.arvionbasket. It is provided by ARVION / Łukasz Bednarski. There is no ARVION account, cloud leaderboard or developer-operated game server in this version.

Information stored on your watch

The game saves your best score, successful shots, unlocked cosmetic choices, language, difficulty, and selected ball and court in private application storage on your watch. The developer does not receive these gameplay records.

60-minute trial and tester access

The 60-minute trial starts when the app is installed and continues while it is closed. Installation time, the highest observed device time, an expiry flag and integrity-protected local records help prevent resetting the trial by changing the clock. The app-scoped Android ID is read locally for an integrity calculation and is not sent to an ARVION server. A tester PIN can enable separate local test access; it does not represent a Google Play purchase.

Google Play purchases and third-party services

The full game can be unlocked with a permanent, non-consumable, one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. The app checks product details and purchase status, receives purchase records and tokens from Google Play, acknowledges completed purchases, stores a local unlock flag and checks Play again when restoring purchases. The developer does not receive card numbers or banking credentials. Google Play and its supporting services may process transaction, app, device, network and diagnostic information and retain their own purchase records according to Google's policies.

Permissions, advertising and offline play

The game does not include ads, subscriptions or developer-installed advertising or behavioural analytics SDKs. It does not request access to your location, health data, contacts, camera, microphone or personal media files. 3D graphics are rendered locally. Gameplay can work offline while the trial or a cached full-version entitlement is valid, but buying or restoring the game requires Google Play connectivity.

Security, retention and deletion

Local records are protected by Android's application sandbox and remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Automatic app-data backup and device-transfer backup are disabled in this build. Clearing data removes local progress, trial information and the cached unlock; a qualifying purchase may be restored from the same Google Play account. Android system or testing logs may contain technical diagnostics but are not automatically sent to an ARVION server. Google manages retention of its own payment records.

Your questions and policy updates

For privacy questions or applicable data-protection requests, contact the developer by email below. The developer has no separate ARVION user-account database to delete; records stored by Google are managed by Google. Do not send card details or purchase tokens by email. This policy may be revised if the app's data practices change; the revision date will appear on this page.

Privacy contact: lukaszbednarski998@interia.pl
