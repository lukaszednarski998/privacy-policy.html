# Privacy Policy – Checkers Royal Game

**Application:** Checkers Royal Game  
**Package name:** `com.aplikacjeandroid.checkersroyalgame`  
**Effective date:** 26 August 2026

Checkers Royal Game respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how the current version of the game handles user information.

## 1. Personal data

Checkers Royal Game does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to play. The game does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data with the developer.

## 2. Local game data

Game settings, scores, progress and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on the user's device. This information is used only to provide game functionality.

## 3. Device permissions

Checkers Royal Game does not require access to contacts, precise location, microphone recordings, photos, messages or other personal content for its core gameplay.

## 4. Google Play and platform services

The game may be distributed through Google Play. Google Play and the Android operating system may independently process technical, diagnostic, purchase-related or account-related information according to Google's own terms and privacy policies. Such processing is controlled by Google and not by Checkers Royal Game.

## 5. Data sharing

Checkers Royal Game does not sell personal or sensitive user information. The developer does not intentionally share personal or sensitive user information with third parties.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game data remains on the device until the user clears the application's data or uninstalls Checkers Royal Game. Users can remove locally stored data through Android application settings or by uninstalling the game.

## 7. Security

Checkers Royal Game is designed to minimize the processing of personal information. Local data is protected by the security mechanisms of the user's Android device and operating system.

## 8. Children's privacy

Checkers Royal Game does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children. The game does not require an account or personal information to play.

## 9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this address.
