Privacy Policy - CAM 2 WATCH

Effective date: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to CAM 2 WATCH for Android phones and Wear OS smartwatches, package name pl.arvion.spy2watch.

Summary: CAM 2 WATCH lets a user intentionally stream live camera video and microphone audio from an Android phone to a paired Wear OS smartwatch. The app can also save recordings locally on the phone. CAM 2 WATCH does not contain advertising, does not use developer-operated analytics or cross-app tracking, and does not require an ARVION account.

1. Camera and microphone access

CAM 2 WATCH uses the phone camera and microphone only after the user starts a streaming session and grants the required Android permissions. These permissions are necessary to provide live video, live audio, camera switching, microphone control, and optional recording.

CAM 2 WATCH does not secretly activate the camera or microphone from a locked or idle phone. While a camera or microphone session is active, Android may display a persistent foreground-service notification and system privacy indicators.

2. Live streaming to the smartwatch

During an active session, camera video and microphone audio may be transmitted from the phone to the user's paired Wear OS smartwatch so the user can view and control the live session from the watch.

Depending on the selected connection mode, streaming may use the local network, Wi-Fi, phone hotspot, Wear OS communication services, or an Internet/LTE relay configured by the user.

3. Internet/LTE relay mode

CAM 2 WATCH can optionally use a WebSocket relay server address configured by the user. If the user enables this mode, live video, live audio, connection status, and technical session data may pass through that relay server.

The developer does not require a developer-operated CAM 2 WATCH cloud account or central media-storage service for normal use. If the user chooses a third-party or self-hosted relay server, that server is outside the developer's direct control and may process data according to its own configuration and privacy practices.

4. Recordings

If the user starts recording, CAM 2 WATCH may record camera video and microphone audio and save the resulting media file locally on the Android phone.

On supported Android versions, recordings are stored in the user's shared media storage under Movies / CAM 2 WATCH. Because these are user media files, they may remain on the device after the application itself is uninstalled until the user deletes them.

CAM 2 WATCH does not automatically upload locally saved recordings to a developer-operated server.

5. Local app data

The app may store local settings and operational state needed to provide its features, including selected language, camera preference, microphone state, video quality, connection mode, relay address and token entered by the user, purchase entitlement state, trial timing information, and other technical preferences.

This data is used to operate the application and is not intentionally used by the developer for advertising or behavioral profiling.

6. 60-minute trial

CAM 2 WATCH may provide a free trial of approximately 60 minutes from first installation. The app stores information needed to determine whether the trial is active or expired, including installation-related timing and entitlement state.

Trial information is used only to manage access to the trial and full version of the app.

7. One-time purchase and Google Play Billing

CAM 2 WATCH may offer a permanent full-version unlock through a one-time purchase in Google Play. Payment processing is handled by Google Play Billing.

CAM 2 WATCH does not receive or store payment-card numbers or other payment credentials. The app may receive technical purchase and entitlement information from Google Play, such as whether the full version has been purchased and identifiers needed to confirm, acknowledge, or restore the purchase.

Google may process account, transaction, device, security, and billing information independently under Google's own terms and privacy policies.

8. Wear OS and Google Play services

CAM 2 WATCH uses Android, Wear OS, Google Play services, and related platform communication features to connect the phone and smartwatch and exchange control/status messages.

Google and device manufacturers may independently process technical information necessary to provide those platform services. Such processing is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Permissions and device capabilities

Depending on Android version and enabled features, CAM 2 WATCH may request or use camera, microphone, Internet/network access, notifications, foreground-service capabilities, and wake-lock functionality.

These permissions and capabilities are used to provide live streaming, recording, phone-watch communication, user-visible foreground operation, and related app functionality.

CAM 2 WATCH does not require access to contacts, SMS messages, precise location, health records, or advertising identifiers for its core functionality.

10. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

CAM 2 WATCH does not contain third-party advertising, behavioral advertising, developer-operated analytics, or cross-app advertising tracking.

11. Sale or sharing of personal data

The developer does not sell or rent personal data collected through CAM 2 WATCH.

Live media is transmitted only as required for the user's selected phone-to-watch connection mode. If the user chooses an external relay server, data necessary for that relay connection may be transmitted to that server as described above.

12. Data retention and deletion

App preferences, trial information, and local entitlement state are generally stored on the device and may be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application, subject to Android and Google Play behavior.

Recordings saved in the shared Movies / CAM 2 WATCH folder are user media and may remain after uninstalling the app. Users can delete these recordings from CAM 2 WATCH, the device's media/gallery tools, or a compatible file manager.

Google Play may retain purchase and transaction records according to Google's own legal and service requirements.

13. Security

CAM 2 WATCH is designed to minimize unnecessary data processing and to keep recordings on the user's phone unless the user chooses otherwise. Live streaming is limited to the active connection selected by the user.

No electronic storage or network transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should use trusted networks and trusted relay servers when enabling Internet/LTE relay mode.

14. Children's privacy

CAM 2 WATCH is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children. Users should not use the app to record, transmit, or store content unlawfully or without any consent required by applicable law.

15. User responsibility and lawful use

CAM 2 WATCH is a camera and recording utility. Users are responsible for using the application in accordance with applicable privacy, recording, surveillance, workplace, and consent laws. The app should not be used to record or monitor people where doing so would be unlawful.

16. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights concerning personal data, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and other rights provided by local privacy law.

Because CAM 2 WATCH is designed not to maintain a developer-operated user-profile database, the developer may not possess normal locally stored app data or recordings. For privacy questions or requests relating to the app, please use the official developer contact information displayed on the CAM 2 WATCH Google Play listing.

17. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if CAM 2 WATCH functionality, platform services, permissions, billing, streaming methods, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.

18. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to CAM 2 WATCH, please use the official developer contact information shown on the app's Google Play listing.
