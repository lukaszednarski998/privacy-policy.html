PHONE CONTROL 2 WATCH PRO
PRIVACY POLICY

About the app
PHONE CONTROL 2 WATCH PRO is a companion app for Android phones and Wear OS watches. It lets the owner approve one paired watch to request supported commands and selected files. Remote access is disabled by default and does not require a separate developer account.

Data handled by the app
The app locally stores a paired Wear OS node identifier and display name, authorization setting, trial/purchase state, selected document names and content URI references, a single synchronized note, and downloaded files on the watch. When requested, it processes the phone model, battery level, charging state and whether a network connection is available. It does not provide access to all private phone files or read arbitrary messages.

Permissions and optional features
Internet connectivity supports communication between devices. Only files selected in the Android file picker can be shared. The optional notification permission enables the Find phone alert. If you grant notification listener access, the app locally stores up to 30 recent notification previews (app name, title, text and time) and shares them only on request with your authorized watch. You may clear this history or revoke access in Android settings. Notification access also allows compatible media controls. Individual contact names and phone numbers chosen through the Android contact picker are stored locally and available to your authorized watch; the app does not request unrestricted contacts permission. Opening the system camera app does not give this app camera access. Microphone and precise location permissions are not requested.

External services and sharing
Commands, notes and selected file content are transmitted between the phone and watch using the Google Play services Wearable Data Layer. Depending on connectivity, Google infrastructure may relay this data and process associated technical information under its own privacy terms. Google Play Billing processes in-app purchase details. We do not operate a separate cloud account, advertising network, analytics service or developer-hosted storage server for these app features; we do not sell user data.

Storage and deletion
Local preferences, notes, selected-document references and downloaded copies remain on devices until the user removes them, clears app data or uninstalls the app, subject to Android backup/restore settings. Removing a document or disabling remote access prevents new requests but does not automatically erase copies already saved on the watch. Files on the watch are removed when app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Google independently controls any data retained by its services.

Security and limits
Only the specifically authorized paired watch may send commands to the phone. Data transport and app identity are managed by Android/Google Play services and app signing. The developer does not claim independent end-to-end encryption beyond the protections supplied by those services. Do not share sensitive files without considering connected-device and Google-service security.

Your choices
You may keep remote access disabled, revoke watch authorization, remove shared documents, replace or delete a synchronized note, and clear local app data. Android settings let you withdraw notification-related permissions. There is no developer-managed user account to delete. The app is not designed for unattended surveillance.

Privacy contact
For privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on the app’s Google Play listing or the public support issue link: https://github.com/lukaszednarski998/privacy-policy.html/issues/new. Policy version: 11 October 2026.

権限

外部サービス

セキュリティ
承認された時計だけが操作を送れます。通信とアプリ署名の保護はAndroidおよびGoogle Play開発者サービスに依存します。独自のエンドツーエンド暗号化を保証するものではありません。

ユーザーの選択
