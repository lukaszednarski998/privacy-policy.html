PET EVOLUTION 3D — Privacy Policy

PET EVOLUTION 3D is a virtual-pet game for Android phones and Wear OS smartwatches. This policy explains the game data handled on your devices and through supported platform services.

APLIKACJE ANDROID · Android + Wear OS · com.arvion.petevolution3d · 2026-11-01

1. Data stored on your device

The game stores the selected pet, any name you give it, care statistics, health and energy, experience, levels, in-game progress, room and decoration selections, sound and other settings, and timestamps needed to update your pet. These records are kept in the app's local storage. You do not need to create a developer account.

2. Synchronization between phone and watch

When supported and enabled, the game can exchange pet progress and technical synchronization information, including an installation identifier, between your paired Android phone and Wear OS watch using Google Play services / Wearable APIs. The developer does not operate a separate game-account server or cloud profile database for this feature. Platform services may process technical communication data under their own policies.

3. Permissions, reminders and backups

The Android project declares internet access for platform communications, notification permission for optional pet-care reminders, and boot-completed access to reschedule reminders. Notifications can be declined or disabled in system settings. The game does not require contacts, camera, microphone or precise-location access for gameplay. Android backup and device-transfer services may copy selected local game settings and progress, depending on your device and account settings.

4. Payment and advertising

PET EVOLUTION 3D is offered as a paid game through Google Play, without a free trial, advertisements or a recurring subscription. Google Play manages the purchase and payment transaction. The game does not request or store your payment-card details. Purchases made through Google Play are subject to Google's own terms and privacy practices.

5. Third-party services and disclosure

The reviewed game project contains no advertising SDK and no developer-operated analytics service. The game uses Android, Google Play services and Wear OS functionality where needed. The developer does not sell player data. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may process website access information separately; no analytics scripts have been added to this policy page.

6. Retention, deletion and choices

Local gameplay data remains until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Clearing data or uninstalling can permanently remove progress. Copies may remain on a paired device or in a platform-managed backup; delete or manage those separately through the relevant device or Google settings. If the game offers synchronization controls, you may disable synchronization in its settings.

7. Children and security

The game does not ask players to provide their real name, address, contact details or other sensitive information. Do not enter personal data as a pet name. Game records are stored using Android application storage and device-to-device communication relies on platform services; no electronic storage or transmission method is absolutely secure.

8. Your rights, contact and updates

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to information, access, correction or deletion of personal data. Since ordinary gameplay records are stored on your devices, you can manage them using the controls described above. For privacy requests, use the official developer contact listed on the game's Google Play page; do not post sensitive information in public GitHub issues. This policy may be updated when game functionality or legal requirements change. Last updated: 1 November 2026.
