Privacy Policy · English

ARVION Retro Snake is a standalone game for Wear OS smartwatches. It can be played offline and does not require an account with the developer.

Last updated: 22 October 2026  ·  Application ID: com.aplikacjeandroid.arvion.retrosnakewear
Platform: Wear OS  ·  One-time product: full_version

1. Gameplay and local data

The game saves your best score, selected speed, edge-wrap setting and local full-version access status on your watch. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts or location to play.

2. 60-minute trial and device information

The 60-minute trial starts when the app is installed and continues while the game is closed. To check its integrity, the app reads the installation time, device clock information and Android ID. Android ID is used locally when calculating a signature of the trial state; the app does not send it to the developer.

3. Google Play purchase

The full game can be unlocked with a one-time purchase (product ID: full_version). Google Play handles payment. The app receives purchase status, product identifier and a purchase token when needed to acknowledge or restore the purchase. The app does not receive your bank-card details.

4. Third-party services

The game does not include advertising, developer-operated analytics, a developer login or a developer-operated game server. Google Play, Android and Wear OS may separately process device, account, diagnostic and transaction information when providing their services. Their processing is governed by Google's policies. Google Privacy Policy ↗

5. Permissions and backup

The game requests vibration access for haptic feedback. It does not request camera, microphone or location permissions. Android backup is enabled; depending on your device and account settings, local game and trial data may be backed up to or restored from Google services.

6. Storage, removal and your choices

Local game settings, scores and trial records remain on the device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to backup and restoration. Clearing local data does not delete purchases recorded by Google Play. You can manage app data in watch settings and backups or purchases through your Google account.

7. Children and security

There is no in-game chat or developer account, and the game is not designed to collect children's personal information. Parents should supervise purchases. The app relies on Android's local app storage and Google Play safeguards; no digital system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes and contact

We may revise this policy when the game's functions or service integrations change; the current version will remain available at this URL. For privacy questions, use the developer contact details in Google Play or the official ARVION website. ARVION ↗
