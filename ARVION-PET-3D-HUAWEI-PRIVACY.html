Privacy Policy - ARVION PET 3D

Effective date: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to ARVION PET 3D for Huawei smartwatches, package name com.arvion.pet3d.huawei.

Summary: ARVION PET 3D is designed to work locally on the user's smartwatch. The app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

1. Data collection

ARVION PET 3D does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, health information, advertising identifiers, or payment information.

2. Local game data

The app stores game information locally on the user's device so that gameplay can continue between sessions. This may include the chosen pet, the custom pet name entered by the user, pet status and needs, level and experience, selected room, gameplay settings, action cooldowns, and timestamps used to update the virtual pet over time.

This local game data is used only to provide the game's functionality. It is not intentionally transmitted to the developer or to external servers by ARVION PET 3D.

3. Permissions

The app is designed to use only permissions necessary for its smartwatch functionality. It does not require access to contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, photos, or health data for its core virtual-pet gameplay.

4. Advertising, analytics and tracking

ARVION PET 3D does not include third-party advertising, behavioral advertising, developer-operated analytics, or cross-app tracking.

5. Generative AI

ARVION PET 3D does not provide or use a generative AI service.

6. Sharing and sale of personal data

The developer does not sell or rent personal data collected through ARVION PET 3D. Because the app is designed not to transmit personal data to the developer, there is no developer-operated database of ARVION PET 3D user profiles.

7. Children's privacy

The app does not intentionally request personal information from children. Users should not enter personal or sensitive information as a pet name or in any other user-editable field.

8. Data retention and deletion

Game progress and settings are stored locally on the user's device. Users can remove locally stored app data by using the device's available app-data removal/uninstallation functions. The developer does not retain a separate server-side copy of this local game data.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights concerning their personal data, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and other rights provided by local privacy law. ARVION PET 3D is designed so that the developer does not receive personal data from the app. If you have a privacy question or wish to exercise a privacy right concerning the app, please contact the developer using the official developer contact information displayed on the ARVION PET 3D AppGallery listing. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

10. Platform services

Huawei/AppGallery and the smartwatch operating system may process information independently when providing platform services such as app distribution, installation, updates, security, or store functionality. Such processing is governed by the relevant platform provider's own privacy terms and is separate from data processing by ARVION PET 3D.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or legal requirements change. The current version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to ARVION PET 3D, please use the official developer contact information shown on the app's Huawei AppGallery listing.
