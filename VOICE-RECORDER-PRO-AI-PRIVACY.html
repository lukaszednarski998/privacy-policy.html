Privacy Policy – VOICE RECORDER PRO AI

Effective date: 26 September 2026

1. Scope and developer

This Privacy Policy applies to the VOICE RECORDER PRO AI Android and Wear OS application (package: pl.arvion.voicerecorder). The application is provided under the ARVION brand. Privacy contact: obdactiveexhaust@gmail.com.

2. Data processed by the app

The app can process audio recordings, recording titles, dates, duration, audio parameters, source information, folders, tags, favorites, trash status, markers, waveform cache data, transcripts, summaries, task text, edit history, settings and offline speech-model files. These data are stored in the app’s private storage on the user’s device unless the user explicitly exports, shares, backs up or transfers them.

3. Audio recording and microphone

Microphone access is used only for recording features started by the user. Recording runs as a foreground operation with Android’s notification mechanisms where required. The app does not contain a silent call-recording mechanism and does not access call logs, contacts or location.

4. Transcription and AI features

Transcription is designed to work locally. The app includes offline Vosk-based transcription and, on supported Android versions, may use Android’s on-device speech recognizer with offline preference. Audio is not automatically uploaded by the developer for transcription. If the user opens the external Vosk model website, that website is outside the app and is governed by its own privacy terms.

5. Phone and Wear OS synchronization

The Wear OS version can record audio on the watch and transfer selected recordings to a paired phone. Synchronization uses Google Play services / Wear OS Data Layer. Data involved in that transfer may be handled by Google’s infrastructure according to Google’s terms and privacy policies. The developer does not operate a separate cloud service for storing those recordings.

6. Sharing, export and backups

The user can explicitly share recordings or transcripts, export files, choose an external destination using Android’s system document picker, and create manual ZIP backups. Once the user sends or saves data outside the app, the selected third-party app, storage provider or destination may process that data under its own privacy policy. Manual ZIP backups may contain readable audio and metadata and are not encrypted by this app.

7. Purchases and internet access

The app may use Google Play Billing for a one-time purchase and Google Play services for Wear OS connectivity. The INTERNET permission supports those services and opening user-selected external resources. Purchase processing is performed by Google Play. The app may locally cache purchase verification data needed to confirm entitlement. The developer does not receive or store the user’s payment-card details.

8. Advertising, analytics and developer cloud

This source version does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, a developer-operated cloud inference API, or automatic upload of recordings to a developer server.

9. Permissions

Depending on device and feature use, the app can request microphone, notifications, Bluetooth connection, foreground-service, wake-lock, audio-settings and Internet permissions. It does not request broad external-storage access, contacts, location, call-log or accessibility-service permissions.

10. Storage, retention and deletion

Recordings and related app data remain on the device until the user deletes them, clears app data or uninstalls the app. Items moved to the app’s trash remain there until permanently deleted. Android automatic app backup is disabled. Reinstalling the app or clearing app data can remove local recordings, models, settings and cached entitlement information. Files exported, shared or backed up outside the app must be deleted separately from the destination chosen by the user.

11. Security

The app stores its internal data in Android app-private storage and does not allow Android’s standard automatic backup. Users should protect their device and treat exported recordings and unencrypted ZIP backups as sensitive files.

12. Support communications

If a user contacts the developer by email, the developer may receive the information included in that message, such as the sender’s email address, message content and attachments, solely to respond to the inquiry and handle support. Email is processed through the relevant email service provider.

13. Children

The app is a general-purpose recording utility and is not specifically directed to children. The developer does not knowingly operate a server that profiles children or collects their recordings.

14. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its permissions, third-party services or legal requirements change. The current version will be published at this URL.
