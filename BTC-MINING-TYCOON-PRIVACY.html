1. Scope and publisher

This policy describes privacy matters relating to the game BTC MINING TYCOON, published by Łukasz Bednarski STUDIO / APLIKACJE ANDROID. It covers gameplay information, potential platform interactions, and the public policy webpage. Contact the developer through the contact information listed on the game's official Google Play store page or the developer website.

2. Game content is simulated

BTC MINING TYCOON is a simulation game about building GPU mining rigs, operating a virtual mining facility, managing power, conducting research, and trading simulated BTC on in-game exchanges. The BTC balances, exchange quotations, currencies and profits displayed in gameplay are virtual game values, not real cryptocurrency, financial assets, or guaranteed earnings. The game is not a cryptocurrency wallet, exchange, or real Bitcoin mining service.

3. Information used to run the game

Depending on the installed version and its enabled features, the game may maintain information necessary for play, such as virtual currency balances, equipment and room configurations, progress, upgrades, tasks, research, and settings. Such information is primarily game-state information; if saved on the device, it can normally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. The developer has not supplied an audited final build for this policy, so this document does not claim that every game-state item is exclusively local or that no network requests occur.

4. Google Play and third-party services

If the distributed version offers purchases, Google Play Billing may process transactions, purchase entitlements, payment and account information under Google's own policies. The developer does not need your payment card number to provide an in-game purchase. Android and Google Play may also process technical information relating to installation, updates, diagnostics, security, and optional device backups. Read the Google Privacy Policy. Whether the final game uses any additional SDKs, online services, crash reports or analytics must be checked against the actual release build and disclosed here if present.

5. Advertising, tracking and permissions

No advertising or behavioral-tracking functionality is represented in the gameplay materials supplied for preparation of this policy. However, the absence of advertisements, analytics SDKs, tracking identifiers, and sensitive permissions has not been independently verified against the final application package. Before publication, the developer must make sure this policy, Android permissions and the Google Play Data safety disclosure agree with the released version.

6. Sharing, retention and deletion

Gameplay records stored in the app's local storage can generally be deleted through Android Settings → Apps → BTC MINING TYCOON → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the game. This may erase progress and preferences. Platform transaction history and any backups are managed separately by the relevant provider, for example Google. The developer should revise this section if a version introduces accounts, cloud saves, developer-hosted servers, or external data sharing.

7. Security and privacy rights

We aim to use appropriate safeguards for information handled by the game; no technical system is completely risk-free. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing of personal data. For developer-controlled data or privacy questions, use the developer contact details on the official website or the Google Play listing. Requests about Google-controlled account and transaction records should be directed to Google.

8. Children

The game is not designed as a service for collecting children's personal information. Parents or guardians should use Google Play parental controls to supervise purchases. Any age-related handling and declarations must reflect the actual game's intended audience and SDK behavior.

9. Hosting of this webpage
