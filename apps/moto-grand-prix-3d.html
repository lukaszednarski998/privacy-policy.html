MOTO GRAND PRIX 3D

3D motorcycle racing, time attack and a night burnout arena. Phone and Wear OS.

Coming soon

MOTO GRAND PRIX 3D brings motorcycle racing and tire-smoking stunts to Android phones and Wear OS watches. Race against computer-controlled riders, chase your own lap records or leave the starting grid behind for a floodlit burnout arena. Choose a motorcycle in the 3D garage, learn the circuits and decide whether the next session is about a clean racing line or another figure eight drawn in rubber.

FOUR WAYS TO RIDE
Quick Race puts you into a race against AI opponents. Championship connects successive rounds with points and progression. Time Attack gives you space to work on braking, corner exits and your personal best lap. Burning Rubber changes the pace: enter a night arena through its gate and practice without a lap target or a race finish. The arena is surrounded by tire barriers, stands and lighting, with stars above the riding area.

10 CIRCUITS AND 12 MOTORCYCLE VARIANTS
Learn ten circuits and browse twelve motorcycle variants in the garage. Earn virtual credits through race results, unlock additional bikes and tracks, and keep building your local progress. Three difficulty levels let you adjust the opposition. The variants use different model and color combinations; this is an arcade motorcycle game with its own circuits.

ACCELERATION, BRAKING AND BURNOUTS
Use the throttle, steering and brakes to control your ride. A separate front brake adds another way to slow down and, with enough speed, raise the rear wheel for a stoppie. Hard acceleration can lift the front wheel; ease the throttle or brake to bring it down. Activate burnout controls, turn either way and join circles into figure eights. Tire smoke and rubber marks follow the rear wheel, while exhaust flames and animated smoke add to the action. These are game mechanics for virtual riding and stunts.

A GARAGE WITH SOMETHING TO DO
Preview and rotate your motorcycle, change variants and confirm your selection. Rev the engine, try a garage burnout and flash the motorcycle lights and indicators. The 3D space includes a display platform, ceiling lights, wall pictures and workshop scenery. Engine recordings, tire sounds and visual effects accompany the bike both in the garage and on the track.

RACE INFORMATION AT A GLANCE
Follow speed and engine revs on the gauges, check the minimap and watch your position and lap information. Circuit races include a pit entry, a pit-lane speed limit and a service stop. Pause when you need a break, then return to the same session. Adjust sound, music and control settings to suit the way you play.

PHONE AND WEAR OS CONTROLS
The phone edition supports portrait and landscape layouts with touch steering and throttle control. On Wear OS, automatic acceleration leaves more room for steering arrows and compact gauges. Optional wrist steering includes calibration and saved sensitivity. Additional controls sit in a bottom drawer; supported watches can also use rotary or bezel input. The phone and watch editions run independently, with local saves rather than shared online progress.

LOCAL PLAY, CLEAR ACCESS RULES
Gameplay works offline and contains no advertising. Purchases and purchase restoration use Google Play and may require an Internet connection. The interface supports English, Polish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Turkish, Indonesian, Russian and Ukrainian.

A 60-minute trial starts at installation and continues even while the game is closed. After it expires, continued access requires a one-time full-version purchase through Google Play. There is no subscription. Some motorcycles and circuits are unlocked with virtual credits earned in the game.
