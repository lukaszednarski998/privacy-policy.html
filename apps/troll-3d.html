TROLL 3D

Raise a 3D monster, feed it meat and make mischief in spooky rooms.

Coming soon

Adopt a little monster and raise it in TROLL 3D. Care for a virtual creature in a spooky world filled with eerie rooms, meaty meals and mischievous play.

Meet 13 monsters with their own species names and distinct voice banks. Give your companion a name, watch its needs and see it grow. Animated 3D creatures walk, jump, react to touch and take part in care activities.

• Serve four kinds of chunky meat meals.
• Choose four 3D toys and make mischief.
• Use Mess up to scatter papers and rubbish around the room. The litter disappears after a few seconds. Toy play scatters litter too.
• Explore eight spooky rooms and a fantasy acid shower.
• Manage food, energy, happiness, cleanliness and health. Earn experience and decorations through care.

Play on an Android phone or a supported Wear OS watch. Each device keeps its own local save. Core play works offline; purchases and restoration require Google Play.

TRIAL AND FULL GAME
Try the game and all 13 monsters for 60 minutes from the first launch. Time continues while the app is closed. One purchase unlocks the full game after the trial, with no subscription. The current price comes from Google Play and is displayed when purchasing. Your progress remains saved when the trial ends.
