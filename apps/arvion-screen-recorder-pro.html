ARVION Screen Recorder Pro

Record Android and Wear OS screens, control remotely, share MP4 videos.

Available now on Google Play

Open in Google Play

Record your Android phone screen and control screen recording from your Wear OS smartwatch. On compatible watches, you can also capture the watch display itself. ARVION Screen Recorder Pro brings an MP4 screen recorder, two-way remote controls and a video gallery together in one connected experience.

Create app demonstrations, capture on-screen steps, make short tutorials or save moments you want to review. Start a recording on either device and manage it without constantly switching between your phone and watch.

Key features:
• Record Android screen videos as MP4 with START, PAUSE, RESUME and STOP controls.
• Two-way remote recording: control the phone from your Wear OS watch or control the watch from your phone. Choose local, remote or both devices.
• Built-in recording gallery to play, rename, delete and share videos with supported Android apps.
• Transfer MP4 files between phone and watch. Wear OS services use an available Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or network route, with percentage progress and an option to cancel a transfer.
• Adjust recording quality and frame rate (FPS); optionally include microphone audio after granting permission.
• Recording-session notifications and local access to saved clips on both devices.

Videos are stored locally. For cross-device controls and file transfer, install ARVION Screen Recorder Pro on your Android phone and a compatible Wear OS smartwatch, then connect them through Wear OS services.

A 60-minute trial starts at installation. Unlock the full version with one in-app purchase. No ads and no subscription.

Please note: Android requires user approval for screen capture. Protected content may appear black in recordings. Watch-screen capture depends on the specific Wear OS model, operating system and video encoder support. Remote features require an available connection between the devices.
