VIDEO 2 WATCH makes it easy to browse videos stored on your Android phone, search your library and play selected content on a compatible Wear OS smartwatch.

The app is designed for users who want a simple phone-to-watch video experience without complicated setup. Connect your devices, choose a video and stream or transfer it to your smartwatch.

MAIN FEATURES

  • browse videos stored on your Android phone
  • search your personal video library
  • select videos for playback on your smartwatch
  • stream or transfer selected videos to a compatible watch
  • Bluetooth support for device connection
  • Wi-Fi and local network support where available
  • simple phone-to-watch connection process
  • clear full-screen interface
  • fast access to your media library
  • modern dark interface for everyday use
  • no third-party advertising
  • no subscription required

VIDEO 2 WATCH focuses on direct communication between your phone and smartwatch. The app does not require you to upload your personal video library to an external developer-operated cloud service.

Your videos remain under your control. Media access is used to show your video library and let you choose content for playback, streaming or transfer.

The app may request access to videos stored on your device, Bluetooth, nearby devices and network connectivity when those permissions are needed for phone-to-watch communication.

Playback quality can depend on the smartwatch model, Android and Wear OS version, available memory, connection quality, video resolution and supported video formats.

VIDEO 2 WATCH is built to make watching your own videos on a smartwatch easier and more convenient. Choose your language, connect your watch, select a video and enjoy your content directly on your wrist.
