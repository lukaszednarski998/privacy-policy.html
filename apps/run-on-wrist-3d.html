RUN ON WRIST 3D

3D adventure runner for Android and Wear OS. 10 worlds, traps and treasure.

Coming soon

An adventure that fits on your wrist. RUN ON WRIST 3D brings temple running to Wear OS smartwatches and Android phones. Choose an explorer, head into ancient ruins and see how far you can go before the traps bring your expedition to an end. Every stretch asks you to read the route, make a decision and time your next move.

PLAY ON YOUR WATCH OR PHONE
The Wear OS edition is a standalone smartwatch game. Running does not require a phone app to stay open or a permanent connection to a phone. On Android, the same adventure uses a larger display, supports portrait and landscape play and includes a large, live 3D character preview. Pick the device that suits the moment.

THREE LANES AND QUICK DECISIONS
Your character runs automatically while you choose a route through three lanes. Swipe left or right, jump over low obstacles and slide beneath suspended traps. Optional on-screen buttons provide another way to play. Speed builds gradually, making a clear view of the road ahead just as valuable as a fast reaction.

WRIST MOTION CONTROLS
On a compatible watch, tilt your wrist to change lanes, jump or slide. Hold still at the start of a run to establish your neutral position, then return to the center between gestures. Adjustable sensitivity helps you find a comfortable response. Touch controls remain available, and holding the pause icon lets you take a break on the watch.

TEN WORLDS TO EXPLORE
Enter Lost Jungle Temple, Desert Tomb and Volcanic Sanctum. Continue through Frozen Citadel, Sunken Ruins and Sky Bridges. Discover Cursed Swamp, Crystal Caverns, Canyon of Kings and Storm Altar. Different backgrounds, materials, palettes and musical tracks give each destination its own atmosphere.

WATCH THE TRAPS MOVE
Avoid spikes rising from inset traps, wooden beams and spiked obstacles. Rolling boulders approach your character, while hanging beams and chain balls sway from supporting frames. A continuous road connects the generated sections. The changing layout rewards attention rather than memorizing one short course.

TREASURE AND PERSONAL BESTS
Collect coins and diamonds to improve your score. A heart protects you from one hit, and a clover briefly doubles the treasure you collect. Distance and pickups both contribute to your result. Your best runs are stored locally, giving every new attempt a target to beat. Restart after a run and try a different route or world.

ANIMATED EXPLORERS AND LIVING SCENERY
Choose Mira or Darius, two animated 3D characters with running, jumping and sliding movements. Burning torches, drifting mist and continuous fire effects add movement to the surroundings. Each world has its own music, while recorded character efforts and movement sounds follow the action. Music and sound effects can be switched off separately.

BUILT FOR YOUR OWN PACE
Take a short run or keep going as the generated road unfolds. Pause when you need a break, change the control sensitivity and choose the graphics frame-rate setting. Local records keep the focus on improving your own performance. There is no game account to create, and the interface supports 14 languages.

NO ADS OR SUBSCRIPTIONS
Try the full game for 60 minutes of active app use. When that time is used, one full-version purchase unlocks continued access. Coins and individual maps are not sold separately. Gameplay works offline; buying, restoring a purchase and periodic entitlement verification require Google Play and an Internet connection.

Choose your world, find your rhythm and chase your next personal best in RUN ON WRIST 3D.
