Retro Snake Classic brings the legendary Snake game to Android with classic arcade gameplay, smooth controls and a nostalgic retro style.

Guide the snake across the board, collect fruit, grow longer and try to beat your high score. The rules are easy to learn, but every move becomes more challenging as the snake grows.

🐍 CLASSIC SNAKE GAME Enjoy familiar Snake gameplay inspired by classic mobile phones and retro arcade games.

🎮 SMOOTH CONTROLS Responsive controls make changing direction quick and intuitive, so you can focus on surviving longer.

⚡ MULTIPLE SPEED LEVELS Choose a comfortable pace or increase the speed for a more difficult challenge.

🏆 HIGH SCORE Your best result is saved, giving you a clear target every time you play.

🔊 SOUND & VIBRATION Sound effects and vibration feedback make fruit collection and collisions more satisfying.

📱 MADE FOR ANDROID Optimized for Android phones, tablets and larger screens, with support for portrait and landscape layouts.

Retro Snake Classic is a simple and engaging arcade game for fans of classic Snake, retro games and score chasing. Collect fruit, grow your snake, beat your record and see how long you can survive.
