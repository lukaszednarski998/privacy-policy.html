Pixel Critters 3D

Raise a 3D virtual pet, decorate rooms, collect rewards and play minigames

Pixel Critters 3D is a colorful virtual pet game where you choose your companion, hatch it, give it a name and build its own little world.

Explore themed rooms, change their furnishings, use the backpack, choose outfits and grow your collection. Play with your critter, feed it, let it rest, and watch its animations and reactions.

The game also includes quests, achievements, rewards and an album. When you want a change of pace, try minigames such as running, fishing, tic-tac-toe, catching eggs and treasure memory.

Key features

  • 3D virtual pets and characters
  • character selection, hatching and naming
  • multiple themed rooms
  • room decoration and item management
  • outfits and customization
  • backpack, album, quests, achievements and rewards
  • several minigames
  • in-game shop with items and optional diamond packs through Google Play
  • full interface in 14 languages

Pixel Critters 3D combines virtual pet care, decorating, collecting and quick minigames in one colorful 3D world.
