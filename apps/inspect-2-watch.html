INSPECT 2 WATCH — Inspection camera preview from phone to Wear OS

INSPECT 2 WATCH is planned as a camera-preview companion between an Android phone and a compatible Wear OS smartwatch. The phone receives the camera image and can send its preview to the watch.

COMING SOON — this app is not available to download yet.

Planned functionality

  • Preview supported USB UVC inspection cameras connected to a phone
  • Use a compatible Wi-Fi camera that provides an MJPEG stream
  • Use the Android phone camera as a video source
  • View a live preview on the phone and compatible Wear OS watch
  • Control supported recording sessions from the paired devices

Device compatibility

USB camera support requires UVC compatibility and an OTG-capable phone or adapter. Wi-Fi cameras must provide a supported video stream. This is not a claim that every camera works.
