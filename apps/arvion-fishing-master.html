ARVION FISHING MASTER

Fishing game for Android and Wear OS: 30 fish, 10 locations and rod upgrades.

Coming soon

ARVION FISHING MASTER brings a colorful fishing adventure to your Android phone and Wear OS smartwatch. Cast your line, watch for a bite, handle the tension and build your own collection of catches. Whether you enjoy relaxing fishing games or short skill-based challenges, every trip gives you another chance to improve your record.

Explore 10 fishing locations
Travel between lakes, rivers, the coast and more. The game features Forest Lake, Rocky River, Mountain Lake, Autumn Pond, Sea Coast, Tropical Lagoon, Winter Lake, Swamp, Moonlit Lake, City Harbor. Each place has its own atmosphere and selection of fish. Start at a peaceful forest lake, then spend your earned coins to unlock new spots with different scenery and species. Try a moonlit session, visit the tropical waters or cast from a city harbor.

Discover 30 species of fish
The fish catalog includes Perch, Northern pike, Zander, Catfish, Carp, Brown trout, Atlantic cod, Tuna, Atlantic salmon and many other freshwater and saltwater species. Find familiar fish alongside harder-to-encounter catches. Species differ in strength, potential weight and rarity, and the fish you can encounter depend on the selected location. Your bait and casting choices also matter.

Fishing that rewards timing
Hold and release to cast your rod, wait for the float to signal a bite and tap at the right moment to hook the fish. During the fight, hold to reel in and release when the line tension gets too high. Reel too aggressively and the line can snap; react too late and a fish may escape. Balance progress and tension to bring your catch safely to shore. The mechanics are approachable for beginners but leave room to sharpen your timing.

Rods, bait and useful upgrades
Choose among 10 fishing rods, including Basic, Spinning, Fly, Carp, Sea, Legend. Compare equipment as you advance, and choose the rod that suits your next fishing trip. There are also 10 bait choices, from natural bait to artificial lures: Earthworm, Corn, Soft Lure, Wobbler, Spoon and other options. Different bait types can change which fish are more likely to bite. Unlock equipment and locations using the virtual coins earned by playing.

Build a fishing collection
A successful catch is recorded in your collection. Check species, see how many you have caught and follow your best weight for each fish. Keep the fish or release it: both decisions save the catch, while releasing gives extra virtual coins. Earn additional coin rewards as your catch count grows, improve your overall level and return to earlier locations to chase a heavier record.

Made for Android phones and smartwatches
Enjoy the same core fishing adventure on a phone or a compatible Wear OS watch. The watch version uses touch-friendly controls and a compact interface for a small, round display. Start a quick fishing session from your wrist, or play on your phone when you want a larger view of the water, dock and underwater fish. Progress is saved locally on each device.

An inviting 2.5D fishing world
Stylized fishing scenes combine animated water, swimming fish, a wooden pier, a small angler and environmental details. Different locations change the mood, from bright coastal water to lakes after dark.

More ways to make the game yours
Track your catches and records, open the fishing equipment gallery and adjust sound effects, music, vibration and battery-saving settings. The game interface supports 14 languages. Core fishing gameplay works offline, without a constant internet connection. No third-party advertising and no subscriptions are included in this build.

If you are looking for a fishing simulator-style game for Android, an offline fishing game with a collection of fish or a fishing adventure you can play on a Wear OS smartwatch, ARVION FISHING MASTER lets you cast, catch, collect and explore at your own pace.
