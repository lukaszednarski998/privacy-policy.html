RC NAVAL WARFARE 3D

3D naval battles with RC ships and submarines. Offline on Android and Wear OS.

Coming soon

Turn a swimming pool into a naval combat arena. Command a destroyer, battleship or submarine among buoys and floating pool equipment. Every vessel has its own HP bar. Damage to propulsion, rudder and weapons affects the fight, so use cover, choose your distance and watch the reload gauge.

Explore 10 large pools, from a volcanic arena and mountain obstacles to a night stadium and a deep submarine hunting ground. Choose from 60 vessels: 36 surface ships and 24 submarines. Three difficulty levels let you start gently or seek a greater challenge. On your watch, play with one finger and check health and reload around the screen edge.

Command RC ship models in battles across large pool arenas. Choose a surface ship or submarine, control propulsion, select weapons and fight AI opponents. Play independently on Wear OS or an Android phone, including offline.
