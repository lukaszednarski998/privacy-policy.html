Offline music on Wear OS — MUSIC 2 WATCH

Send your own music and audio recordings to your watch, then play supported tracks from its local storage without keeping your phone connected. Prepare a playlist for a walk, workout or journey. Music synchronization compares the watch library and sends missing tracks. For playback directly from your phone, connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network and choose streaming.

Videos on your smartwatch — VIDEO 2 WATCH

Copy personal videos to Wear OS for local playback, or stream from your phone while both devices share the same Wi-Fi network. WATCH OPTIMIZED prepares a 480p MP4 copy using H.264 video and AAC audio. This can help adapt a video for the watch; conversion and playback still depend on your devices, codecs and available storage.

Phone camera on your wrist — CAM 2 WATCH

Check your phone camera framing from your watch. Switch between front and rear cameras, control the microphone and use recording controls during a session. Start the camera session on your phone and grant the required permissions. A local Wi-Fi connection or phone hotspot supports live preview; Android displays an active-session notification and camera or microphone indicators.

Two-way file transfer and Browser Share

Send selected files from Android to Wear OS and from the watch back to the phone. Use the Explorer for music, video, photos, PDFs and other documents, select several files or package a folder as a ZIP. Browser Share lets a compatible browser on the same local network download shared files without installing the receiving app. Sending a file does not require the watch to be able to open its format.

How to connect Android and Wear OS

Install ARVION Watch Media Hub on the phone and compatible watch, pair the devices and open the app on both. Select MUSIC, VIDEO, CAM or SEND FILE, then grant access required by that feature. Transfer routes include Wi-Fi/LAN, Bluetooth and supported Wi-Fi Direct hardware. Streaming music and video requires the same Wi-Fi network; a phone hotspot can provide that local connection.

60-minute trial, one purchase, no ads

The app offers a 60-minute trial counted from installation, including time when the app is closed. The full version uses a one-time Google Play purchase, with purchase restoration, no ads and no recurring subscription. Check current availability, device compatibility and purchase terms in Google Play. Local file transfers do not require uploading your library to a developer cloud.

Frequently asked questions

Can I listen to music on Wear OS without my phone?

Yes, supported tracks already copied to watch storage can play locally without an active phone connection. Streaming from the phone and transferring new tracks require a connection.

Can I watch videos without internet?

Compatible videos stored on the watch can play locally. Phone-to-watch music and video streaming uses the same local Wi-Fi network or a phone hotspot; installation and purchase verification may require internet.

Can I send PDF files and photos to my watch?

Yes. Explorer can transfer selected PDFs, photos and other files in both directions. Opening a received file depends on the apps and formats supported by the receiving device.

Which smartwatches are compatible?

The watch app requires a compatible Wear OS device. Check your model in Google Play. Apple Watch and watches with other operating systems cannot be assumed to support the Wear OS app.
