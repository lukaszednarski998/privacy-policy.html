ARVION BASKET 3D WATCH

3D basketball on Wear OS: free throws, 1v1 AI games and dunk challenges.

Coming soon

Looking for a basketball game for your Wear OS smartwatch? ARVION BASKET brings a compact 3D basketball experience straight to your wrist: quick shots, free throws, a 1v1 game against a computer rival and dunk timing challenges. The athlete, ball, court and hoop are rendered in 3D. No phone companion app is needed. Play offline while your 60-minute trial or saved full-game unlock is valid.

Four ways to play
• Quick shot: practise your aim in a timed session.
• Free throws: take five attempts and review your result.
• Street 1v1: face a computer opponent that moves, defends and shoots. Swipe sideways to move and use automatic defence.
• Dunk challenge: tap at the right moment to perform a dunk. Timing accuracy affects the points awarded.

Swipe upwards to shoot. The direction and length of your gesture affect the ball’s path. Ball motion and collisions are calculated in real time. A basket is counted when the ball passes down through the rim, and the net reacts to a score. Pause, resume or restart whenever you need.

Keep a local best score and unlock different balls and court surfaces through successful shots. Choose your difficulty and one of 14 interface languages. An animation viewer lets you inspect the player’s movement clips.

The trial lasts 60 minutes from installation and continues when the app is closed. One permanent Google Play purchase unlocks the full game. No advertisements, subscriptions or paid random loot boxes. Buying and restoring require Google Play connectivity; the store displays the price.

Scores and settings stay on the watch. Read the privacy policy in game settings for details about local records and Google billing services.
