REAL KART RACING 3D

Race five 3D go-karts across 10 offline circuits on Android and Wear OS.

Coming soon

Choose one of five kart-and-driver combinations and race four AI opponents over three laps. The ten circuits include alpine hairpins, a harbor, city streets at night and a wet track with reduced grip.

Choose your difficulty and camera, steer with touch controls, and switch between manual and automatic throttle. Best lap times and podium counts are saved on your device.

Play offline on a compatible Android phone or directly on a supported Wear OS watch. A phone connection is not required during a watch race. Try the game for 60 minutes from installation, then unlock it with a one-time Google Play purchase. Internet access is needed to purchase or restore. No ads or subscriptions.
