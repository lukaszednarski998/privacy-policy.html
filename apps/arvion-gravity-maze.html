ARVION GRAVITY MAZE

Roll a ball through 36 maze levels on Android and Wear OS. Avoid traps.

Coming soon

ARVION GRAVITY MAZE is a ball maze puzzle game for Android phones and Wear OS smartwatches. Guide a metallic ball through winding passages, collect the crystals and find your way to the exit. The rules are simple, but a good run takes careful movement, timing and a little planning.

FIND THE ROUTE, THEN MASTER IT
Each level has a maze to explore and crystals to collect. Gather every required crystal before reaching the exit. Watch the layout from above, choose a path through the corridors and keep the ball away from obstacles that can cost you a life.

Some routes lead to portals that transport the ball to another part of the maze. Spikes and moving hazards demand extra attention: the shortest path is not always the safest one. Learn how the ball responds, slow down when you need precision and work out the right moment to pass a dangerous section.

36 LEVELS, SCORES AND STARS
Explore 36 levels with different maze layouts. Track your time and score as you play. Completing a stage rewards you with a star rating based on how the run went. Retry a level to improve your result, protect your remaining lives and work toward unlocking the next challenge.

TOUCH OR TILT CONTROLS
Drag on the screen to steer the ball, or use tilt controls when your device has a suitable motion sensor. You can switch control methods and adjust sensitivity in Settings. Sound effects, music and vibration feedback can also be turned on or off. Pause the action whenever you need to take a break.

MADE FOR PHONE AND SMARTWATCH
Play ARVION GRAVITY MAZE on a compatible Android phone or directly on a Wear OS watch. The clear overhead maze view helps you identify walls, crystals, portals and traps, even on a compact round display. The core game runs offline; Google Play connectivity may be required for purchases and restoring a previously owned full version.

ONE-TIME UNLOCK, NO ADS
Try the game for 60 minutes from installation. After the trial, the full game can be unlocked through a one-time Google Play purchase. There are no ads or recurring subscriptions. The game includes 14 interface languages.

If you enjoy rolling-ball games, labyrinth puzzles, pathfinding and quick skill challenges, ARVION GRAVITY MAZE gives you a route to solve and a score to beat on your phone or your wrist.
