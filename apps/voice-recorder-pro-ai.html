VOICE RECORDER PRO AI – voice recorder for Android and Wear OS

VOICE RECORDER PRO AI brings together an Android voice recorder, standalone Wear OS recording, audio editing and on-device speech recognition. Capture voice notes, meetings, interviews, lectures, ideas and longer recordings. Keep files in local storage, organize your audio library, edit recordings and export in a suitable format.

Available now on Google PlayOpen in Google Play

What can you record?

  • Capture quick voice memos and ideas without typing.
  • Record lectures, interviews, meetings and longer audio sessions using your device microphone.
  • Keep recordings, time markers, transcripts and audio files organized in one place.

Android phone and Wear OS watch

  • Start and control phone recording remotely from a paired Wear OS smartwatch.
  • Use your watch independently as a voice recorder without first starting a session on the phone.
  • Record on the watch, add markers and browse the watch’s own local recording library.
  • Transfer selected watch recordings to your paired Android phone for playback and further editing.

WAV recording and sound controls

  • Record PCM16 WAV audio, pause or resume a session and insert time markers as you record.
  • View a live audio waveform and actual input signal level.
  • Choose quality presets or manually configure sample rate, channel count and an available audio input.
  • Enable device-provided noise suppression, echo cancellation and automatic gain control when supported.
  • Skip silence and split long recordings into segments when a more manageable result is needed.
  • Recover interrupted WAV sessions when the original PCM data was successfully saved.

Recording library and playback

  • Search recordings by name, folder, tag or saved transcript text.
  • Organize files in folders, apply tags and mark important recordings as favorites.
  • Move recordings to the trash and restore them before permanent deletion.
  • Play audio with seeking, markers and adjustable playback speed.
  • Import compatible PCM16 WAV files, share recordings with other apps, and manually back up or restore the library as a ZIP archive.

Audio editing and export

  • Keep a selected portion of audio or remove a selected segment without recording it again.
  • Merge compatible recordings and normalize audio levels.
  • Export audio as WAV, lossless FLAC, M4A/AAC or OPUS using encoders supported by your device.

Speech to text and local AI tools

  • Transcribe saved recordings with on-device speech recognition on compatible Android versions.
  • Optionally import a Vosk model for private offline transcription.
  • Edit transcripts and save corrected text permanently.
  • Extract a short summary and explicitly stated action items from transcript text.
  • Export or share transcripts as TXT files.

Privacy, trial and one-time purchase

  • Recordings remain in the app’s private local storage unless you choose to export, share, back up or transfer them.
  • The app contains no ads and no subscriptions, and includes a 60-minute trial.
  • After the trial, unlock the full version with a one-time purchase.
  • Noise processing, speech recognition and export formats depend on device and Android feature support.
