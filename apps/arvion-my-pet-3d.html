What is MY PET 3D?

A virtual pet game lets you return to the same animal companion, care for it and interact with its environment. MY PET 3D focuses on that familiar pet-care idea, with a colorful 2.5D look and scenes built for the selected platform.

Your virtual animal companion

A pet-care game with small, interactive activities. Meet your animal, take care of it and spend time in its virtual world.

Explore the pet's rooms

The game presents environments and rooms instead of reducing the experience to a single static pet icon. Room interactions depend on the current game version.

Colorful 2.5D look rather than a pixel-only pet

MY PET 3D uses sprites styled to suggest depth. Its graphics look three-dimensional, but these characters are not continuously rendered polygonal 3D models.

Play on the right device

The Android edition is made for phones; the Wear OS edition adapts virtual-pet gameplay to the smaller smartwatch screen. Check compatibility before installing.

What makes the graphics different?

MY PET 3D uses sprites styled to suggest depth. Its graphics look three-dimensional, but these characters are not continuously rendered polygonal 3D models.

Visuals and supported activities may differ by version. This is a sprite-based 2.5D pet game, not a fully modeled 3D simulation.

Questions about this virtual pet game

Is MY PET 3D a Tamagotchi game?

It belongs to the virtual pet and pet-care genre, so people looking for a Tamagotchi-style smartwatch game may enjoy the concept. It is an independent ARVION game, not an official Tamagotchi product.

Does MY PET 3D use real-time 3D models?

No. This edition uses 2D/2.5D sprites with a 3D-like visual style. The name describes the look, not a real-time 3D rendering engine.

Can I play on a Galaxy Watch or Pixel Watch?

The watch edition targets compatible Wear OS devices. Availability and support depend on the particular model, system version and Google Play device catalog; compatibility is not guaranteed for every watch.

What do you do in the virtual pet game?

You care for an animal companion, explore room-based scenes and use the interactions available in your installed version. The published demonstration shows the game's visual style.

Is the phone version the same as the smartwatch version?

They target different screen sizes. The Android and Wear OS pages describe their respective versions; cross-device syncing or one purchase covering both is not promised here.
