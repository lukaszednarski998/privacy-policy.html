ARVION Battery Guard is a battery monitor and charging analyzer for Android. Track charging power, current, voltage, battery temperature and available battery health data in real time.

Monitor every charging session, receive smart charging alerts, analyze charger behavior and reduce excessive heat and unnecessary battery wear.

⚡ LIVE CHARGING POWER

Monitor charging power, current and voltage while your phone is connected to a charger. See how charging changes with different chargers, cables and conditions.

🌡️ BATTERY TEMPERATURE MONITOR

Track battery temperature while charging and receive warnings when the battery becomes too hot.

🔔 SMART CHARGING ALERTS

Choose your preferred charging limit: 80%, 85%, 90% or 100%. Battery Guard can notify you when the selected battery level is reached.

🛡️ BATTERY CARE & HEALTH

Analyze charging habits and use battery-care indicators together with battery health information available from Android.

📊 CHARGING HISTORY & STATISTICS

Review previous charging sessions, temperatures, charging values, scores and other useful battery statistics.

🔋 BATTERY PASSPORT

View available battery information in one place, including status, temperature, voltage, health and cycle information when supported by your device.

🧪 CHARGER LAB

Analyze charger performance and monitor available charging parameters to better understand how your phone is being charged.

🎧 BLUETOOTH BATTERY CENTER

View available battery information for compatible connected Bluetooth devices.

📄 PDF & CSV REPORTS

Export available battery statistics and charging information to convenient PDF and CSV reports.
