KIDS REMOTE LOCK — Parental controls, screen-time limits and remote lock

KIDS REMOTE LOCK is a parental-control app for Android with remote device locking, screen-time limits and schedules for compatible devices.

AVAILABLE NOW ON GOOGLE PLAY

OPEN IN GOOGLE PLAY ↗

Main features

  • Set screen-time rules for a child's supported device
  • Create schedules and daily usage limits
  • Manage app blocking and device lock settings
  • Use parent-managed protection and pairing
  • Control supported child-device access from a parent device

Device compatibility

Available on Google Play for compatible Android devices. Compatibility depends on the device model and Android version.
