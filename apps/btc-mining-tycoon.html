BTC MINING TYCOON

Build GPU mining rigs, mine virtual BTC and grow your 3D crypto mining base.

Coming soon

BTC MINING TYCOON is a 3D crypto mining simulator where you build GPU mining rigs, manage virtual Bitcoin production and grow your own mining business. Start with a single workstation, choose your hardware and develop a larger mining facility.

BUILD AND ASSEMBLE MINING RIGS
Choose graphics cards and other PC components, assemble mining setups and start your machines. Compare hardware, mining output, power consumption and running costs. Maintain and upgrade your rigs as your operation grows.

TRADE VIRTUAL BITCOIN
Follow simulated BTC prices across several in-game exchanges. Compare rates and decide when to buy or sell virtual Bitcoin. Keep track of income, expenses and the profitability of your mining operation.

EXPAND YOUR 3D MINING FACILITY
Organize workstations, unlock additional rooms and grow your infrastructure. Explore a three-dimensional environment, inspect equipment and plan your next investments.

MANAGE POWER AND RESEARCH
Balance your facility's energy needs. Use in-game solar panels, wind turbines and energy storage systems. Invest in research, technology upgrades and stronger components to improve your mining setup.

MAKE THE BASE YOUR OWN
Develop the grounds around your facility, place vehicles and make decisions about equipment, trading and expansion.

BTC MINING TYCOON combines a Bitcoin mining game, PC building and business management in a detailed 3D tycoon simulation. All cryptocurrency mining and trading in the game are simulated. The game does not mine real Bitcoin or provide real-money payouts.
