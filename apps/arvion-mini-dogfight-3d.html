ARVION MINI DOGFIGHT WATCH

3D air combat on Wear OS. Tiny aircraft, five rooms and 20 missions.

Coming soon

Turn your room into an aerial battlefield. ARVION Mini Dogfight 3D is an aircraft game for Wear OS watches: fly a miniature plane between furniture, chase enemy aircraft and return to the 3D hangar for your next sortie.

AIR COMBAT ON YOUR WRIST
Steer by touch or by tilting your watch. A separate red FIRE button lets you steer and shoot together. Machine-gun ammunition is unlimited; bomb capacity depends on your aircraft. Adjust the volume of engine, gunfire and explosion sounds.

A FLEET WITH DISTINCT STATS
Start with the Falcon A-01. Five additional aircraft are available: Biplane, Bomber, F-16, F-35 and Twinwing. View rotating 3D models in the hangar and compare speed, handling, hull and firepower. Earn points through victories and upgrade your aircraft. Enemies also fly different fleet models.

FIVE ROOMS, FOUR WAYS TO FLY
Explore a kitchen, living room, bedroom, garage and children's room. Play 20 campaign missions featuring patrols, two enemy waves, timed interceptions and ace encounters. Try a quick dogfight, time attack or free flight. Three difficulty settings change enemy behavior and combat strength.

TRIAL AND PURCHASES
The 60-minute active-play trial includes only the Falcon A-01 and kitchen. After the trial, a one-time full-game purchase is required to keep playing. The full game includes the basic aircraft and all five rooms. Each of the five extra aircraft is a separate optional purchase, available only after unlocking the game. Google Play displays prices. Purchase restoration is supported.

For Wear OS watches only. Standalone play; no phone version. Gameplay works offline; purchases and restoration require Google Play and internet access. No ads or subscriptions. Interface available in 14 languages.
