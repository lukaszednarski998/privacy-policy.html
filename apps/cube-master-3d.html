CUBE MASTER 3D

Offline 3D cube puzzles for Android phones and Wear OS watches

Coming soon

Twist cubes and explore spatial puzzles on your wrist. CUBE MASTER 3D is a puzzle game for Android phones and compatible Wear OS watches. Play independently on your watch without needing your phone. Rotate the 3D view, select a move and practice pattern recognition at your own pace.

A collection of 20 puzzles
Explore cubes from the compact 2×2 to the multilayer 7×7, pyramids, polyhedra and shape-changing puzzles. Each type has its own move set. Square-1 blocks cuts through pieces, while Gear Cube couples layer turns with gear rotation.

2×2×2, 3×3×3, 4×4×4, 5×5×5, 6×6×6, 7×7×7, Pyraminx, Master Pyraminx, Skewb, Megaminx, Kilominx, Square-1, Mirror Cube, Fisher Cube, Windmill Cube, Axis Cube, Gear Cube, Ivy Cube, Dino Cube, Redi Cube.

Choose your way to play
Enjoy Free Play, Time Attack, Training, Zen, a local Daily Challenge or a progressive challenge with longer scrambles. Follow your time, move count and local records. Daily scrambles use your device date and need no server.

Clear controls and eight styles
Drag to rotate the camera, then use buttons or a gesture to execute the selected move. Explicit move selection helps on small screens. Adjust materials, sound, vibration, sensitivity and battery saving. Face symbols help distinguish colours.

Progress stored on your device
The game saves your current puzzle, move history and elapsed time. Scrambles create reachable states and Undo reverses the previous move. Hints use your history; they are not a universal automatic solver. Phone and watch progress and records are separate.

Try the game for 60 minutes
The trial lasts 60 minutes from installation, including while the app is closed. Unlock the full game with one Google Play purchase, without ads, subscriptions or paid colour packs. Restore your purchase with the same eligible Google Play account. Buying and restoring require Google Play access; puzzle play works offline.
