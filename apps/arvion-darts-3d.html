ARVION DARTS 3D WATCH

3D darts for Wear OS. Play 301, 501, Cricket and train offline, without ads.

Coming soon

Bring the dartboard to your wrist. ARVION DARTS 3D is a sports game for Wear OS smartwatches. Three-dimensional darts, an illuminated board and a calm club atmosphere keep the focus on your next throw and each three-dart turn.

SIX WAYS TO PLAY
Quick Play gives each player 30 darts to build the highest total. In 301 and 501, count down to exactly zero; enable double out when you want to finish on a double. Cricket challenges you to close 15–20 and bull, with points against open opponent targets in two-player games. Around the Clock takes you through 1–20 and then bull. Practice offers unlimited training turns.

MADE FOR THE SMALL SCREEN
Swipe up to throw. Speed controls power; time your release. Bezel or left arc: power. Touch the board to aim. Tap ↑ to release. The watch works independently. Play solo or share one watch with a second local player.

MAKE EACH TURN COUNT
Review your three-dart average, throw count, hit rate, best turn, last turn and recent history. Local achievements recognize your first completed game, a 100-point turn and the maximum 180. Choose among three dart styles and Club, Ice or Ember environments. Adjust sound, haptics and optional aim assist.

A SIMPLE PREMIUM MODEL
No ads. No subscriptions. ARVION STANDARD TRIAL 60 MIN lasts 60 minutes from installation, including time away from the app. One non-consumable purchase unlocks the full game after the trial. Restore that purchase on a compatible Wear OS watch using the same Google Play account. Gameplay and local statistics work offline; buying or restoring needs Google Play and connectivity. Game updates do not require a subscription.

Select from 14 interface languages and make time for one more round. Whether you want realistic darts, a focused training session or a smartwatch darts game for a short break, ARVION DARTS 3D keeps the dartboard close.
