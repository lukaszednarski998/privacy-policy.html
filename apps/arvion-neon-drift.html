ARVION NEON DRIFT

Neon arcade car racing on Android and Wear OS. Dodge traffic with touch or tilt!

Coming soon

ARVION NEON DRIFT is a fast-paced neon arcade driving game for Android phones and Wear OS smartwatches. Take control of a futuristic car, weave through glowing roads and dodge obstacles as the pace builds. Every lane change is a quick decision: stay alert, pick your opening and keep your run going.

THREE LANES. QUICK REFLEXES.
The road keeps moving and the next obstacle is never far away. Shift left or right to avoid traffic, cones and barriers, then collect shining crystals along the way. Your score increases with distance and successful pickups. You have three lives, so choosing the right moment to change lanes is part of the challenge.

WHAT'S IN THE GAME?
• Three-lane arcade car driving focused on timing and obstacle avoidance.
• Neon scenery that changes between a futuristic city, bridge and tunnel.
• Passing traffic, roadblocks and different types of hazards.
• Collectible crystals and a locally saved best score to chase.
• Useful power-ups: a protective shield, a pickup magnet and a speed boost.
• A garage with four visual car variants to choose from.
• Sound effects, pause and resume controls for your runs.

TOUCH, TILT OR BOTH
Set up the controls the way you prefer. Tap the left or right side of the screen to switch lanes, steer by tilting your phone or watch, or combine both methods. Choose from three tilt-sensitivity levels and calibrate the neutral position whenever you need. Simple controls make it easy to start a round; precise timing is what keeps the car safe.

BUILT FOR PHONE AND WATCH
Play ARVION NEON DRIFT on an Android phone or directly on a compatible Wear OS smartwatch. The phone version uses the larger display, while the watch version is tailored to a compact circular screen. Each device can run its own game without using the other as a remote control. Your score and settings are stored locally on the device you play on.

A LITTLE FURTHER EVERY TIME
Learn to read the gaps between hazards, make the most of a shield when traffic gets crowded and collect crystals without losing sight of the road. Try another round, find your rhythm and see whether your next score can beat your personal best. There are no complicated racing rules: keep moving, react quickly and survive the course.

The interface supports 14 languages. There are no ads and no subscriptions. A 60-minute trial is available, with an optional one-time Google Play purchase to unlock the full game.

If you enjoy arcade racing, neon car games, reflex challenges and obstacle-dodging games, take the wheel in ARVION NEON DRIFT — on your phone or your wrist.
