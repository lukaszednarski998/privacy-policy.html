FORMULA GRAND PRIX 3D

3D formula racing for Wear OS and Android. 10 circuits, AI rivals and a garage.

Coming soon

FORMULA GRAND PRIX 3D brings open-wheel racing to Wear OS watches and Android phones. Wait for the starting lights, find a way past computer-controlled rivals and work on your racing line. The watch version runs independently, so you can race on a compatible Wear OS device without launching the phone game.

TEN CIRCUITS TO LEARN
Explore ten original tracks: Riviera, Silver Valley, Sakura Ring, Dune Circuit, Alpine Run, Harbour Lights, Emerald Park, Red Canyon, Pacific Coast and Metro Grand Prix. Different combinations of corners, straights, surroundings and elevation give you new routes to learn. Some circuits feature a night setting. Discover where to carry speed, when to brake and how to recover after running wide.

RACE, TIME TRIAL AND PRACTICE
Race against nine AI-controlled cars in the race mode. Choose your overtaking opportunity and watch for contact with another car or a barrier, which costs speed. Time trial lets you concentrate on your own run, while practice gives you room to learn a circuit. Best lap times and statistics are stored locally. Return to a familiar track and try to improve your previous result.

CONTROLS FOR A SMALL SCREEN
Automatic acceleration and corner assistance help you focus on positioning the car. Touch the left and right controls to steer and hold the brake when you need to slow down. Pause the race when you want a break. Switch between a chase camera and a cockpit view to choose your perspective. On a compatible watch, the rotary crown helps you scroll lists and change garage selections.

A FORMULA CAR IN A 3D GARAGE
Choose from ten color liveries of the same detailed car model. Inspect your car on a three-dimensional platform surrounded by ceiling lights, service crew, equipment and racing pictures. Hold the throttle, trigger a tire burnout with spins or flash the lights. Smoke, tire marks and exhaust effects accompany the garage display. Before the next start, change the livery, circuit or game mode.

LIVE RACING INFORMATION
The speedometer and tachometer show live readings. The HUD also displays a gear indicator, lap count, race position and elapsed time. Recorded engine audio, tire sounds and contact effects accompany the action. Adjust sound, haptics, camera and graphics quality in the options to suit your device and preferences.

PHONE OR WATCH, FOURTEEN LANGUAGES
The interface supports Polish, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Turkish, Indonesian, Russian and Ukrainian. Gameplay runs locally and does not need a continuous Internet connection. Progress is stored separately on each phone or watch, so each device keeps its own results and settings.

TRIAL AND FULL VERSION
The game includes a 60-minute trial measured from installation. Time continues to pass when the app is closed; this is not a counter of 60 minutes of active driving. After the trial expires, continued play requires a one-time full-version purchase through Google Play. There are no ads or subscriptions. Purchase and restoration may require an Internet connection and the appropriate Google Play account. Availability depends on device compatibility.

Pick a circuit, line up at the lights and find out how much time you can take off your next lap.
