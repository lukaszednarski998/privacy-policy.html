MUSIC 2 WATCH is designed for Android phone and Wear OS users who want convenient access to their music directly from their wrist.

The app lets you browse music stored on your phone, select your tracks, and use playback, transfer, and streaming features with a compatible Wear OS smartwatch.

Main features

  • access music stored on your Android phone
  • connect with a compatible Wear OS smartwatch
  • transfer selected music tracks to your watch
  • stream music from your phone to your watch
  • convenient playback controls
  • clean and easy-to-use interface
  • multilingual support
  • no advertising
  • no mandatory subscriptions

MUSIC 2 WATCH provides a simple connection between your Android phone and your Wear OS smartwatch. Select your music on the phone, connect your devices, and enjoy convenient access from your wrist.

The application may use Wi-Fi, local network connectivity, and communication technologies provided by Android and Wear OS to transfer or stream music between your devices.

Your music stays under your control. MUSIC 2 WATCH does not upload your music library to advertising servers and does not use your media for user profiling.

Buy the app and use its features without advertising and without recurring subscription fees.
