MONSTER MOTO 3D

Monster trucks, motorcycles and 3D room jumps on Android and Wear OS.

Coming soon

Turn your room into a miniature driving playground! MONSTER MOTO 3D is a 3D racing game for Android phones and Wear OS watches. Drive monster trucks, cars and motorcycles around household obstacles. Climb ramps, launch jumps and find your own way across the room.

SMALL VEHICLES, BIG ROOMS
Explore five settings: bedroom, living room, kitchen, office and workshop. Furniture, ramps and obstacles become a course from a tiny vehicle’s point of view. Steer left or right, brake, reverse and choose your speed before landing.

BUILD YOUR GARAGE
Browse 23 vehicle models on a 3D garage platform. Earn coins through play, unlock more vehicles and upgrade the engine, grip, suspension, air control and nitro. The full game keeps vehicle and upgrade progression part of gameplay.

CHOOSE HOW TO PLAY
• Career: take on 60 tracks and earn stars.
• Stunt mode: jumps, flips and bonuses for landing on your wheels.
• Time attack: improve your personal records.
• Free ride: pick your own direction and explore without being returned to the course.

Play independently on a compatible Wear OS watch. Progress stays on each device and does not sync between phone and watch. Gameplay works offline; purchases and purchase restoration use Google Play.

Try the game features for 60 minutes of total foreground app use. Then a one-time purchase unlocks continued play without a time limit. No ads, subscriptions or paid coin packs. Use the same Google Play account on phone and watch to restore your purchase.
