SEA GRID: NAVAL BATTLE

Naval grid battles for Wear OS and Android. Play offline AI or duel on Wi-Fi.

Coming soon

Command your fleet in SEA GRID: NAVAL BATTLE, a turn-based naval strategy game with a classic 10×10 grid. Jump into a quick battle with automatic deployment or set up your own fleet for a classic match. Play on Android or on a standalone Wear OS watch.

Place your ships by hand or use automatic deployment. Choose a target, fire and track hits, misses and sunken ships. Face three AI difficulty levels or pass one device back and forth in a local two-player duel. Track wins and accuracy, and set sound and haptics to your preference.

Your fleet has ten ships: one four-cell ship, two three-cell ships, three two-cell ships and four one-cell ships. Drag the zoomed grid in any direction.

Play across two devices on the same Wi-Fi network. Create a room and share its code to play between phones, between watches, or between a watch and a phone.

Try the game for 60 minutes from installation. Unlock the full game with a one-time Google Play purchase. No ads or subscription.
