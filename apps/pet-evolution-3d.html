PET EVOLUTION 3D – A VIRTUAL ANIMAL GAME FOR WEAR OS WATCHES

Turn your smartwatch into a little world of animal companions. PET EVOLUTION 3D is a virtual pet care game designed for Wear OS, where your animal is represented by an animated three-dimensional model instead of a flat picture. Choose a companion, give it a name and enjoy the routine of looking after a pet right on your wrist.

REAL ANIMAL SPECIES, INTERACTIVE 3D MODELS

Explore 3D animal models inspired by species found in nature, including a cat, capybara, bison, squirrel, bee and ladybug. Watch your companion move and react, with animations for blinking, eating, bathing, dancing, resting, playing and showing happiness. This is an interactive animal-care experience, not a gallery of static wildlife images.

CARING FOR YOUR COMPANION

• Feed your animal when it gets hungry and follow its response.
• Play together to keep your companion engaged and cheerful.
• Pet your animal, help it relax and make time for sleep.
• Wash, bathe and clean to take care of your pet's surroundings.
• Check hunger, energy, happiness, cleanliness and health.
• Earn experience points (XP), progress through levels and build your pet's story.

A PET THAT GROWS WITH YOUR ATTENTION

Choose your animal companion, give it a name and care for it day after day. Its needs change as time passes, so paying attention matters. Return to see how your companion is doing, decide what it needs and keep its routine in balance. The game is about nurturing a virtual animal rather than simply collecting points.

ROOMS, ATMOSPHERE AND PERSONALIZATION

Discover different room scenes, change the surroundings and explore lighting options and decorative elements. Adjust interface styles and settings to suit the way you play. Sound, vibration and power-saving options help you tailor the experience to your watch.

MADE FOR A SMALL WATCH SCREEN

PET EVOLUTION 3D is built around Wear OS interactions: touch your pet, pick a care activity, view its status and watch the 3D animation directly on your smartwatch. Controls and menus are arranged for compact screens. You can enjoy a quick moment of play and come back later for another care session without launching a phone game.

A VIRTUAL PET SIMULATOR FOR ANIMAL LOVERS

If you enjoy nurturing pets, watching animal animations, decorating small spaces and making steady progress, PET EVOLUTION 3D brings those activities together in a watch-focused game. Explore a selection of recognizable animals and keep track of each companion's progress. The interface supports 14 languages.

PET EVOLUTION 3D is a paid Wear OS game with no free trial, no advertising and no subscription. Choose your animal companion and take the world of 3D virtual pet care onto your wrist.
