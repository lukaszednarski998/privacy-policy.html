Privacy Policy — ARVION MINI DOGFIGHT 3D
Last updated: 28 October 2026

Developer: Łukasz Bednarski (ARVION)
Contact: lukaszbednarski998@interia.pl
Application package: com.aplikacjeandroid.arvionminidogfight

1. Scope This policy covers ARVION MINI DOGFIGHT 3D, a standalone Wear OS game. The game does not require an in-game account and does not provide public chat or user-to-user messaging.

2. Gameplay and settings stored on your device The game stores settings, selected language, sound volume, control preferences, aircraft and room selection, gameplay progress, scores, mission results, remaining trial time and content-unlock status locally in the application's private storage. Temporary flight state can also be saved to restore a paused game after an interruption. The game does not upload this gameplay information to a developer-operated server.

3. Motion controls When wrist-tilt controls are enabled, the game reads the watch's gravity or accelerometer sensor to steer the aircraft and calibrate the neutral position. These readings are processed locally for controls. The developer does not transmit or retain a history of these sensor readings. Touch controls are also available. The game does not access heart-rate or other health sensors.

4. Trial and owner/tester access codes The game maintains a local trial-usage counter. Where an owner or tester PIN feature is available, it stores the number of failed attempts and the resulting unlock or lockout state locally. The entered PIN is not saved as plaintext in the game's settings; the application contains a verifier. These records are used to enforce trial and access rules and are not sent to a developer-operated server.

5. Google Play purchases The game uses Google Play Billing for a one-time full-game unlock and separate optional one-time aircraft purchases. Prices, payment confirmation and payment processing are provided by Google Play. The game does not request or directly receive card numbers, banking credentials, BLIK codes or PayPal credentials. To deliver and restore purchased content, the game processes purchase information returned by Google Play, including product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase status, acknowledgement status and related receipt/signature data. Entitlement information may be cached in private application storage for offline access. The game requests current ownership from Google Play and acknowledges completed purchases. Pending payments do not unlock purchased content. This version does not send receipts or purchase tokens to a developer-operated verification backend. Google may process account, transaction, device and technical information under Google's own policies and terms. Google Play payment records are controlled by Google, not by the developer.

6. Advertising, analytics and permissions The game does not include advertising, subscriptions, a developer-operated analytics service or a developer-operated crash-upload service. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or personal media files. Network access is used for Google Play purchase services and external links you choose to open. Technical logs may exist locally on the device; they are not automatically uploaded to the developer by this game. Google Play and its services may process their own diagnostic information.

7. Sharing and external services The developer does not sell personal information. The game has no gameplay-data sharing function or developer-operated cloud profile. Google Play services handle distribution and purchases. Opening this policy or another external page creates a connection to the relevant host; this public page is hosted by GitHub Pages. This page contains no advertising scripts, analytics scripts or custom tracking cookies. Google and GitHub process information according to their own privacy policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

8. Retention and deletion Local game data remains until you clear the application's storage or uninstall it, subject to the operating system's behaviour. Clearing local data also removes local settings, trial/PIN state and cached entitlements. Purchases may be restored through Google Play using the purchasing account. Uninstalling the game does not delete Google's transaction records; manage those records and your Google account through Google. If you contact the developer by email, the developer receives the address and information you choose to send and uses it to respond to your request. Such correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. For requests about data under the developer's control, including applicable access, correction or deletion rights, contact the email above.

9. Children and families The game does not ask players to submit personal information through an in-game account or chat. Purchases are handled by Google Play and may be subject to parental approvals and purchase controls. A parent or guardian may contact the developer about information voluntarily provided through a support request.

10. Security and updates Application data is kept in the operating system's private application storage. Purchase communications use Google Play's services. No electronic system can promise absolute security. If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date. Questions about this policy can be sent to lukaszbednarski998@interia.pl.
