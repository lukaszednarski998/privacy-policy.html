# Privacy Policy – VIDEO 2 WATCH

**Effective date:** 26 August 2026  
**Package name:** `pl.arvion.video2watch`

VIDEO 2 WATCH respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information and device permissions.

## 1. Personal data

VIDEO 2 WATCH does not require users to create an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal data for advertising or profiling purposes.

## 2. Video and media access

The application may request permission to access video files stored on the user's Android device. This access is used only to display the user's video library and allow the user to select videos for playback, streaming, or transfer to a compatible smartwatch.

VIDEO 2 WATCH does not upload the user's video library to a developer-operated cloud server.

## 3. Bluetooth and nearby devices

The application may use Bluetooth and nearby-device permissions to discover, connect to, and communicate with a compatible smartwatch or other user-selected device.

Bluetooth access is used only for functions initiated by the user, such as device discovery, connection, streaming controls, or file transfer.

## 4. Wi-Fi and network access

VIDEO 2 WATCH may use Wi-Fi or local network connectivity to stream or transfer video between the user's phone and a compatible smartwatch.

Network communication is used to provide the requested device-to-device functionality. The application does not use this connection to sell or transmit personal information to advertising companies.

## 5. Local storage

Application settings, selected language, device preferences, transfer information, and similar operational data may be stored locally on the user's device.

This information is used only to provide application functionality.

## 6. Data sharing

VIDEO 2 WATCH does not sell user data and does not share personal data with third parties for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

When the user chooses to send or stream a video to a smartwatch, the selected media is transmitted to the device selected by the user as part of the application's core functionality.

## 7. Advertising

VIDEO 2 WATCH does not display third-party advertising.

## 8. Children

VIDEO 2 WATCH is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The application does not operate a user-account system or developer-operated database containing children's personal information.

## 9. Security

The application is designed to keep media access and application settings on the user's device and to use device-to-device communication only when required by the user-selected function.

Users should keep their Android device, smartwatch, and operating systems updated and use trusted Bluetooth and Wi-Fi connections.

## 10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application changes or when legal or Google Play requirements change. The current version will remain available at this GitHub location.
