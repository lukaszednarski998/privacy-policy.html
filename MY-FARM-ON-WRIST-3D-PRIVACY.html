English — Privacy

MY FARM ON WRIST 3D works offline without a game account. The game has no advertising, advertising identifier, analytics SDK or developer-operated game server. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or health access.

Farm progress, optional farm and animal names, inventory, settings and language are stored in the app’s private storage. Names need not identify a real person. Local installation timestamps and a device-bound integrity value support the 60-minute trial, counted from installation including time outside the game. Trial expiry preserves the farm.

Google Play Billing handles the one-time purchase and restoration. The app processes purchase status and the purchase token when communicating with Google Play, and keeps a local entitlement status for offline access after verification. The developer does not receive card details. Google’s purchase records and platform processing follow Google’s policies.

Optional device transfer sends a complete farm snapshot through Google’s Wear OS Data Layer only when you choose to send it. Delivery may be queued until a compatible paired device connects. Import requires a choice, replaces rather than combines progress and keeps a local backup. Purchase entitlement and trial are excluded from farm transfers and exported files.

Android may back up permitted app data according to your device settings. Files you export are managed by the storage provider you choose. These copies and queued device transfers are separate from local farm reset. Reset preserves trial and purchase status. To remove app data, use Android app storage controls or uninstall; manage exported and operating-system backups separately. Google retains purchase records under its own rules.

The game does not ask children for personal information. Local data remains until removed by the user or operating system. The developer does not hold a server copy of the farm. The public policy is hosted by GitHub Pages; visiting it is subject to GitHub’s website processing. Privacy questions can be sent to the contact below; information you voluntarily send by email is used to answer your request.

Local tester access and the count of incorrect PIN attempts are stored on the device. After three errors, the PIN entry option is hidden. Tester access is separate from a Google Play purchase. The PIN and tester status are excluded from farm files and optional farm transfers.
