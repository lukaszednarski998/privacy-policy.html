Privacy Policy – SPY 2 WATCH

Effective date: 27 August 2026
Package name: pl.arvion.spy2watch

SPY 2 WATCH respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Android phone application and its Wear OS companion use the camera, microphone, local storage, network communication, foreground services, and device-to-device communication.

1. Personal data and accounts

SPY 2 WATCH does not require a user account. The developer does not use the application to collect names, postal addresses, contact lists, precise location, advertising identifiers, or other personal information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or behavioral tracking.

The application does not include third-party advertising SDKs or developer-operated analytics SDKs.

2. Camera and microphone

SPY 2 WATCH requests access to the phone camera and microphone because these functions are required to provide live video and audio from the user's Android phone to the user's connected Wear OS smartwatch and to create recordings when the user chooses to record.

The camera or microphone may continue operating while the phone screen is off when a user-requested live session or recording is active. Android foreground services and a system notification are used for this functionality as required by the operating system.

Camera and microphone access is used only for application functions initiated by the user from the phone or connected smartwatch.

3. Live video and audio transmission

SPY 2 WATCH can transmit live camera frames, microphone audio, and necessary session/control information between the user's Android phone and Wear OS smartwatch.

When local communication is available, the application may use Google Play services for Wear OS and a local network/Wi-Fi connection to communicate directly between the user's own devices.

Live video and audio are processed for the purpose of displaying and playing the requested stream on the connected smartwatch. The application itself does not use live media for advertising or profiling.

4. INTERNET / LTE relay mode

SPY 2 WATCH also provides an optional INTERNET / LTE relay mode. This mode is not enabled with a fixed developer server by default. The user supplies or configures a secure WebSocket relay address beginning with wss://.

When this mode is enabled, live video, live audio, device/session status, control messages, and a generated connection token may be transmitted through the relay server configured by the user so that the phone and smartwatch can communicate over the Internet.

Important: the privacy and retention practices of a relay server depend on the operator of that server. Users should only use relay servers they trust. If a third-party relay server is used, that third party may process transmitted data under its own terms and privacy policy.

The SPY 2 WATCH application does not intentionally create a permanent cloud copy of the live stream. However, the developer cannot control or guarantee the behavior, logging, security, or retention practices of a relay server selected by the user.

5. Recordings and local media storage

When the user starts recording, SPY 2 WATCH can create video recordings containing camera video and, when enabled, microphone audio.

On supported Android versions, recordings are saved locally on the user's phone in the device media library, including the Movies/SPY 2 WATCH location. Older Android versions may use compatible local storage mechanisms.

Users can view and delete recordings using application functions or Android system tools. The developer does not automatically upload locally saved recordings to a developer-operated cloud storage service.

6. Phone and smartwatch communication

The application uses Google Play services for Wear OS communication between the user's phone and compatible smartwatch. Communication may include connection status, camera selection, microphone state, recording state, transport settings, session information, control commands, and live media required to provide the requested functionality.

The Wear OS component is a companion application and is intended to work with the user's Android phone.

7. Locally stored settings

SPY 2 WATCH may store application preferences locally on the user's devices. These may include selected language, connection/transport mode, user-configured relay URL, a randomly generated relay token, and other operational settings necessary to remember application configuration.

These preferences are stored in application-local storage and are not used for advertising or profiling.

8. Internet and network access

The application requests Internet/network access to support phone-watch communication, local network streaming, Google Play services for Wear OS, and the optional INTERNET / LTE relay mode.

Network access is not used to serve third-party advertisements.

9. Foreground services, notifications, wake lock, and data sync

On Android, foreground service permissions for camera and microphone are used to keep a user-requested live stream or recording active in accordance with Android requirements. Notification permission may be used to display the required foreground-service notification.

Wake-lock functionality may be used while an active stream or recording is running so that the connection is not interrupted by normal device sleep behavior.

On Wear OS, a foreground data-sync service may be used during an active live connection to keep the requested stream and phone-watch data exchange functioning reliably.

10. Third-party platform services

SPY 2 WATCH uses Google Play services for Wear OS communication. Google and the device operating system may independently process limited technical information required to provide platform connectivity and system services. Such processing is governed by the respective platform provider's privacy terms.

The application also uses standard networking software libraries to establish network and secure WebSocket connections. These libraries do not, by themselves, create an advertising or analytics profile of the user.

11. Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell personal data and does not share user data with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing companies.

Information is transmitted only as required for application functionality selected by the user, such as communication between the user's phone and smartwatch or through a relay server configured by the user.

12. Advertising and subscriptions

SPY 2 WATCH does not display third-party advertising and does not use advertising trackers.

13. Security

The application is designed to limit camera, microphone, media, and network use to its core functions. Internet relay addresses must use secure WebSocket connections beginning with wss://.

Users should keep Android, Wear OS, Google Play services, and their devices updated, use trusted paired devices and networks, protect their phone and smartwatch with device security, and use only trusted relay servers.

14. User choices and control

Users can grant or revoke camera, microphone, notification, and other application permissions through Android system settings. Revoking required permissions may prevent some features from working.

Users can stop active streaming or recording from the application, delete local recordings, switch to local-only communication mode, remove a configured relay server, clear local application data, or uninstall the application.

15. Responsible and lawful use

SPY 2 WATCH is intended to be used only on devices owned or lawfully controlled by the user and only where the user has the legal right and any required consent to capture, record, monitor, or transmit video and audio.

Users are responsible for complying with applicable privacy, recording, surveillance, workplace, and communications laws in their country or region.

16. Children's privacy

SPY 2 WATCH is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The application does not operate a user-account system or a developer-operated database of children's personal information.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality changes or when legal, platform, or Google Play requirements change. The current version will remain available at this public GitHub Pages address.
